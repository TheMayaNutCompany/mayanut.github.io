About The MayaNut Company

Built around MayaNut™ Flour & Powder.

The MayaNut Company was founded by August Kokus to introduce MayaNut™ Flour & Powder as a clear, everyday food ingredient for baking, smoothies, oatmeal, warm drinks, bowls, and modern kitchens.

Our goal is simple: to use MayaNut™ Flour & Powder to help expand the long-term presence, recognition, and practical use of Brosimum alicastrum.

On this site, MayaNut™ refers to the product. The tree, plant, fruit, and seed source are referred to as Brosimum alicastrum.

Our Story

The MayaNut Company was built around a straightforward idea: introduce a roasted ingredient people can actually use at home and give it a clear identity. MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is positioned for practical daily uses such as smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, baking, and warm drinks.

At the same time, the company keeps the source language disciplined. The product is MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. The species behind it is Brosimum alicastrum. That makes the site easier to understand for customers while keeping the botanical reference accurate.

Founder

August Kokus founded The MayaNut Company to create a disciplined, consumer-facing identity around MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. The company’s public language is intentionally precise: MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is the product name used on the site, while Brosimum alicastrum names the tree and seed source.

That product-first approach is meant to reduce confusion, make the site easier to navigate, and give first-time visitors a clear answer to what they are buying and how they can use it.

How We Talk About It

The MayaNut™ name refers to the product MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. It does not refer to the tree, seed, or species in general.
The tree and species are referred to as Brosimum alicastrum. When needed, the site also refers to roasted seeds of Brosimum alicastrum as the ingredient source.
Because the site is selling and explaining a product. Clear naming makes the product easier to understand, while the botanical reference remains accurate and consistent in the background.

Why This Company Exists

The MayaNut Company exists to make MayaNut™ Flour & Powder legible, useful, and desirable as a real ingredient, not just a curiosity. A strong product identity can create repeated kitchen use, and repeated kitchen use can create broader interest in the species behind it.

That is why the About page stays grounded in both sides of the project: consumer clarity around MayaNut™ Flour & Powder as the product, and ongoing awareness of Brosimum alicastrum as the source.
