About The MayaNut Company

Founder story, what MayaNut™ flour is, what Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is, our goal, and launch updates. For product updates visit product.

Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by botanist August Kokus. He started the company to build something lasting—an American-grown food tree story that could outlive any one person, while restoring a truly underdeveloped crop to modern kitchens.

After years of studying plants and pursuing business, August focused on a single question: what if one resilient tree could deliver real nutrition, real flavor, and real cultural history—yet still be almost unknown in the United States? That question became the foundation of The MayaNut Company.

What is MayaNut™ and what is the Alicastrum tree?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—a tropical tree in the same family as figs and jackfruit. When the seeds are roasted and milled, they produce a warm, satisfying flavor with a gentle cocoa-like depth that works in smoothies, baking, and everyday recipes.

Alicastrum has been valued for generations as a dependable food tree. Today, we see it as a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity: a long-lived, climate-resilient species with enormous potential—still early enough that the United States has barely developed it.

Our goal

Our goal is to make MayaNut™ truly popular—so it becomes normal for people everywhere to enjoy it, cook with it, and recognize it as a delicious staple, not a novelty. That means building awareness, building trust, and building supply the right way: carefully, transparently, and for the long term.

We are working to grow and expand Alicastrum in the United States, develop consistent quality standards, and help create a new category around roasted seed flour—one that tastes great, fits modern diets, and supports resilient agriculture. If we do this right, MayaNut™ can become a product people keep coming back to for decades.
