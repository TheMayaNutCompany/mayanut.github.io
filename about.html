About The MayaNut Company

Founder story, what MayaNut™ flour is, what Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is, our goal, and launch updates.

Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by botanist August Kokus. He started the company to build something lasting—an American-grown food tree story that could outlive any one person, while restoring a truly underdeveloped crop to modern kitchens.

After years of studying plants and pursuing business, August focused on one question: what if one resilient tree could deliver real nutrition, real flavor, and real cultural history—yet still be almost unknown in the United States?

What is MayaNut™ and what is the Alicastrum tree?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—a tropical tree in the same family as figs and jackfruit. When the seeds are roasted and milled, they create a warm, satisfying flavor with a gentle cocoa-like depth for smoothies, baking, and everyday recipes.

Alicastrum has been valued for generations as a dependable food tree. Today, we see it as a climate-resilient species with enormous potential—still early enough that the United States has barely developed it.

Our goal

Our goal is to make MayaNut™ truly popular—so it becomes normal for people everywhere to cook with it and recognize it as a delicious staple, not a novelty. That means building awareness, building trust, and building supply the right way for the long term.

We’re working to expand Alicastrum in the United States, develop consistent quality standards, and help create a new category around roasted seed flour—one that tastes great, fits modern diets, and supports resilient agriculture.

Updates

We plan to begin selling MayaNut™ products soon, but we are not announcing a specific date yet. We’re finalizing production, packaging, and quality consistency so your first experience is exactly what it should be.

As we approach launch, we’ll share updates here and across our channels. For questions, email info@mayanut.com.
