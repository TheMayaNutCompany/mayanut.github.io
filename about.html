About The MayaNut Company

Bringing MayaNut™ Flour & Powder to a broader audience.

The MayaNut Company was started by August Kokus to introduce MayaNut™ Flour & Powder as a clear, useful, and distinctive everyday ingredient while helping more people recognize the value of Brosimum alicastrum.

For August, expanding the product is also a way to expand the species. Every serious use of MayaNut™ Flour & Powder can help bring attention, demand, and long-term agricultural interest to the Maya nut tree and its botanical source, Brosimum alicastrum.

The story

A company built around a species, not just a product.

The company’s purpose is simple: make MayaNut™ Flour & Powder understandable, useful, and desirable enough that more people ask where it comes from. That question leads to the Maya nut tree, to the seed, and to Brosimum alicastrum as a species worth noticing, planting, and protecting through practical use.

MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is presented as a branded food ingredient, while “Maya nut” may be used more broadly in reference to the common-name and botanical context around Brosimum alicastrum. That distinction matters because the brand exists to organize the product clearly while still pointing back to the living tree.

Mission

Bring something new and beautiful into the world.

August started The MayaNut Company to bring MayaNut™ Flour & Powder to a bigger, broader audience and to make the product feel new, beautiful, and real in the modern food world.

The larger aim is not only sales. It is the expansion of Brosimum alicastrum through food demand, responsible sourcing, future planting, and long-term public awareness.

Agriculture

Focused on sustainable agriculture practices.

The MayaNut Company is focused on sustainable agriculture practices and long-term expansion of Brosimum alicastrum in places where the tree can grow well.

One future goal is to help establish Florida’s first Maya nut orchard, creating a living agricultural example of the species and its potential in warm-climate food systems.

Inspiration

Music, consciousness, and the will to create.

August was deeply inspired by Scriabin’s Second and Third Symphonies, especially the way they can feel like an expansion of consciousness and a demand to do something special in the world.

He also admired the idea in Isaiah 43:19 of doing something new. For The MayaNut Company, that means building a product, a brand, and a long-term agricultural direction that did not meaningfully exist in this form before.

Name

Why The MayaNut Company?

The name is direct because the mission is direct. The company exists to make MayaNut™ Flour & Powder easier to recognize, easier to understand, and easier to use.

The brand name helps separate the company’s product presentation from the broader common-name phrase “Maya nut,” while keeping the product connected to Brosimum alicastrum and its wider cultural and botanical history.
