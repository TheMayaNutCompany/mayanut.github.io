About The MayaNut Company

Building a long-term, tree-based food and reforestation industry around Brosimum alicastrum in Florida.

The Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by August Kokus. August is currently raising 200 MayaNut seedlings (Brosimum alicastrum) to establish the species in Florida as a new agricultural crop. August knew he needed to achieve something great in the world—and wanted to leave proof of his existence through something that can adapt alongside humanity: something new, original, and worth cherishing. That’s MayaNut.

What is MayaNut?

MayaNut is the seed of the plant Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical rainforest tree in the fig/jackfruit family Moraceae. The tree has been valued for millennia by Indigenous peoples in Central and South America, as well as the Caribbean.

Today, it is making a comeback and gaining popularity among health-conscious consumers. It’s an unexplored flavor worth saving and savoring—one that can support long-term food systems while also fitting into reforestation and canopy-based agriculture.

Building an industry

Developing a new crop takes time: seed-grown diversity, selection, and real-world performance data. Early work focuses on learning what thrives in Florida—growth rate, cold response, productivity, and seed quality—so the strongest trees can guide future propagation.

As parts of Florida agriculture evolve, perennial tree crops like MayaNut can help build resilient systems: food value, shade and canopy benefits, and a long-term pathway toward a dedicated MayaNut farm.

Mission

The mission is to advance cultivation and awareness of Brosimum alicastrum in the United States, starting in Florida, while contributing to research, selection, and responsible expansion in other suitable regions.

The current phase is practical and measurable: build planting stock, document performance, share updates, and prepare the groundwork for Florida’s first dedicated MayaNut farm and future food products.
