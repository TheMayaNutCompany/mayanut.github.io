About The MayaNut Company

The MayaNut Company is building a modern food brand around Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—the tropical food tree whose roasted seeds become MayaNut™ powder, a smooth ingredient with warm roasted depth and a gentle cocoa-like note for smoothies, bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

Our focus is quality, clarity, and long-term responsible growth around a remarkable tree and the foods it can inspire.

Founder + how it started

Founder

August Kokus is the founder and CEO of The MayaNut Company, Inc. He founded the company in October 2025 to help develop Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) as a modern food tree and introduce MayaNut™ to American kitchens.

That work grew out of a long-running fascination with collecting, shaping, refining, and studying things closely—from coins and carved walking sticks to trees, seeds, and plant form. In 2020, that curiosity expanded into deeper plant exploration and long-term observation, eventually leading to a focused interest in Alicastrum and its unusual potential as both a food source and a lasting legacy crop.

The MayaNut Company was built from that attention to detail: not just to sell a product, but to create a durable identity around a tree, a food, and a future-oriented mission. The aim is to turn something underused into something clear, useful, memorable, and lasting.

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Tree

Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is a tropical food tree in the Moraceae family, related to figs and jackfruit. It has a long cultural history and produces seeds that can be dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth ingredient used in both traditional and modern cooking.

Across its range, Alicastrum is known by many regional names, including ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica. These names refer to the same remarkable tree and reflect its wide geographic and cultural reach.

At the center of the story are the seeds: once roasted, they develop a comforting toasted character that translates beautifully into MayaNut™ powder and roasted seed flour. That makes Alicastrum not only culturally significant, but genuinely practical as an ingredient tree for the future.

What is MayaNut™?

Product

MayaNut™ is made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Through careful drying, roasting, and milling, those seeds become a smooth roasted seed powder that carries warm depth, a gentle cocoa-like note, and a rich toasted aroma.

It works naturally in smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, baking, warm drinks, and everyday recipes. The goal is not novelty for its own sake, but a useful, distinctive ingredient people genuinely want to use again.

MayaNut™ is also part of a broader effort to define consistent standards around cultivation, roasting, milling, quality, and presentation so the experience stays clear from tree to finished product.

Mission + legacy

Mission

The long-term mission is to bring Alicastrum and MayaNut™ into wider awareness so more people grow the tree, understand its value, cook with it, and pass it forward. This is not meant as a short-lived trend, but as a durable brand and food identity built to last.

The company’s visual language and symbolism are tied to creation, growth, and brightness. That includes a recurring sense of ascent inspired by Alexander Scriabin’s Symphony No. 3, The Divine Poem—not as endorsement or affiliation, but as an artistic influence on the company’s larger sense of purpose and imagination.

Scriabin is referenced as artistic inspiration only. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
