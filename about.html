About The MayaNut Company

The MayaNut Company is building a modern food brand around Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), the tropical food tree whose roasted seeds become MayaNut™ Flour, a roasted, finely milled powder with smooth texture, warm roasted depth, and a bold cocoa-like flavor for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, oatmeal, and everyday cooking.

The goal is clear standards, strong identity, and long-term growth around a remarkable tree and the foods it can inspire.

Founder

Founder

The Founder and CEO of The MayaNut Company, Inc. is August Kokus. He founded the company in October 2025 to help advance Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) as a modern food tree and introduce MayaNut™ Flour to American kitchens.

August Kokus began with a deep love for plants, but soon recognized the extraordinary potential of one species in particular. The project grew from close study of plants, seeds, cultivation, and product identity, with a focus on transforming an overlooked food tree into a clear, useful, and enduring brand.

Today, The MayaNut Company, Inc. represents a new chapter in Alicastrum’s long history, guided by a long-term mission to help ensure the survival, cultivation, and expansion of Brosimum alicastrum through wider human adoption.

Our Story

Story

The MayaNut Company was built to give Alicastrum and MayaNut™ a durable modern identity. Rather than treating the ingredient as a novelty, the aim is to create a clear standard around cultivation, roasting, milling, presentation, and education so the tree and its food potential can be understood more widely.

The work combines agriculture, branding, research, product development, and long-term planning. It is meant to grow into a lasting company around a useful ingredient and a larger vision for awareness of Alicastrum.

What is the Alicastrum Tree?

Tree

Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is a tropical food tree in the Moraceae family, related to figs and jackfruit. It has a long cultural history and produces seeds that can be dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth ingredient used in both traditional and modern cooking.

Across its range, Alicastrum is known by many regional names, including ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica. These names refer to the same tree and reflect its wide geographic and cultural reach.

At the center of the story are the seeds. Once roasted, they develop a warm toasted character that translates beautifully into MayaNut™ Flour.

What is MayaNut™?

Product

MayaNut™ Flour is made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Through careful drying, roasting, and milling, those seeds become a smooth roasted seed powder with warm roasted depth, bold cocoa-like flavor, and a rich toasted aroma.

It works naturally in smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, baking, warm drinks, and everyday recipes. The point is not novelty for its own sake, but a distinctive ingredient people genuinely want to use again.

Mission + Legacy

Mission

The long-term mission is to bring Alicastrum and MayaNut™ into wider awareness so more people grow the tree, understand its value, cook with it, and pass it forward. This is intended as a durable brand and food identity built to last.

The company’s visual language is tied to creation, growth, brightness, and ascent. That includes a recurring sense of apotheosis inspired by Alexander Scriabin’s Symphony No. 3, The Divine Poem, as an artistic influence on the company’s larger imagination and sense of purpose.

Scriabin is referenced as artistic inspiration only. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
