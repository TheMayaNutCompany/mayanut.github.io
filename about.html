About The MayaNut Company

The MayaNut Company is focused on Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—the tree that produces the seeds used to make MayaNut™ flour, a roasted seed flour for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by botanist August Kokus to develop Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) as a modern food tree and introduce MayaNut™ flour to American kitchens.

We are building clear standards for growing, roasting, and milling so MayaNut™ flour stays consistent—flavor, aroma, and performance in recipes.

What is MayaNut™ flour and what is Alicastrum?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical tree in the same family as figs and jackfruit. Roasting brings out a warm, satisfying flavor with a gentle cocoa-like depth.

Alicastrum is the source of our product. Everything we make starts with the seeds from this tree.

Our goal

Our goal is to make MayaNut™ flour genuinely popular by building trust and supply the right way: consistent quality, clear labeling, and recipes that work.

We are developing Alicastrum as a serious food crop in the United States and helping define a new category around roasted seed flour.

Updates

We plan to begin selling MayaNut™ products soon, but we are not announcing a specific date yet. We’re finalizing inventory, packaging, and consistency.
