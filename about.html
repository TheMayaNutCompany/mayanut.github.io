About The MayaNut Company

The MayaNut Company is focused on Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—the tree that produces the seeds used to make MayaNut™ roasted seed flour for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

Our story

August Kokus is the founder and CEO of The MayaNut Company, Inc. He founded the company in October 2025 to develop Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) as a modern food tree and introduce MayaNut™ roasted seed flour to American kitchens.

From the beginning, August was driven by a collector’s curiosity—collecting coins, carving walking sticks, making perfect spheres out of wood or stone, and channeling that same obsessive attention into plants. In March 2020, with the advent of virtual learning, the creativity that had long felt suppressed by traditional schooling was finally given space to expand. He began discovering and studying a wide range of species around his city, from legumes and Ficus to oaks and other resilient trees.

His love for bonsai led him deeper into Ficus cultivation, which opened the door to the Moraceae family. Seeking something truly original—an overlooked fruiting tree with real agronomic potential—he found Alicastrum. That search became the foundation of The MayaNut Company.

A personal thread of inspiration also shaped the project. August often connects MayaNut to the feeling of creation and “apotheosis” he associates with Alexander Scriabin’s Symphony No. 3 (The Divine Poem)—something rising, unfolding, and becoming. The company’s sun emblem represents that same upward arc: a bright, steady dawn of cultivation and long-term building.

Scriabin is mentioned as artistic inspiration only—no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is a tropical food tree in the Moraceae family, closely related to figs and jackfruit. It is a long-lived, resilient species valued for its dense canopy, ecological importance, and nutrient-rich seeds, which have supported people in the Americas for centuries.

Because of its wide geographic range and deep cultural history, Alicastrum is known by many regional names across countries and languages. Its scientific name is Brosimum alicastrum, and it’s also commonly known as ramón, árbol de ramón, ojoche, guáimaro/guaimaro, capomo, and other local variants used throughout Mesoamerica, Central America, the Caribbean, and parts of northern South America.

Beyond its cultural history, Alicastrum is increasingly recognized as a high-potential food tree for the future: it tolerates tropical heat, supports habitat and shade, and produces seeds that can be dried, roasted, and milled into a satisfying, versatile ingredient. For the MayaNut Company, Alicastrum is both a heritage species and a long-term agricultural focus—grown with consistency, traceability, and modern food standards in mind.

Try MayaNut™ roasted seed flour

MayaNut™ flour is made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Through careful roasting, it develops a warm, satisfying depth with a gentle cocoa-like note and a naturally rich, toasted aroma.

Smooth and versatile, it can be blended into smoothies and açaí bowls, whisked into warm drinks, or used in baking and everyday cooking to add flavor and a unique roasted character—without overpowering other ingredients.

The MayaNut Company is building clear standards for growing, roasting, and milling so MayaNut™ stays consistent in taste, aroma, and performance across recipes.
