About The MayaNut Company

The MayaNut Company is focused on Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—the tree that produces the seeds used to make MayaNut™ flour, a roasted seed flour for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by botanist August Kokus, with a single focus: develop Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) as a modern food tree and introduce MayaNut™ flour to American kitchens.

We believe Alicastrum is an underdeveloped crop in the United States, with real potential as a resilient, long-lived food source—starting in Florida and expanding over time.

What is MayaNut™ and what is Alicastrum?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical tree in the same family as figs and jackfruit. When roasted and milled, the seeds create a warm, satisfying flavor with a gentle cocoa-like depth.

Alicastrum is the source of our product. Everything we make is based on the seeds from this tree—grown carefully, processed thoughtfully, and built for consistent quality.

Our goal

Our goal is to make MayaNut™ flour genuinely popular by building trust and supply the right way: clear standards, consistent roasting and milling, and an honest product people enjoy using every week.

We are developing Alicastrum as a serious food crop in the United States and helping define a new category around roasted seed flour—delicious, versatile, and easy to use.
