About The MayaNut Company

We are building a modern food brand around Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum)—the tropical tree whose roasted seeds become MayaNut™ flour, a smooth ingredient with warm, cocoa-like roasted depth for smoothies, bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

How it started

August Kokus is the founder and CEO of The MayaNut Company, Inc. He founded the company in October 2025 to develop Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) as a modern food tree and introduce MayaNut™ roasted seed flour to American kitchens.

From the beginning, August was driven by a collector’s curiosity—collecting coins, carving walking sticks, making perfect spheres out of wood or stone, and channeling that same obsessive attention into plants. In March 2020, with the advent of virtual learning, the creativity that had long felt suppressed by traditional schooling was finally given space to expand. He began discovering and studying a wide range of species around his city, from legumes and Ficus to oaks and other resilient trees. Our legacy This company is built on a long-term mission: introduce Alicastrum to the modern world so more people cultivate it, cook with it, and pass it forward. August often connects that mission to the feeling of “apotheosis” he associates with Alexander Scriabin’s Symphony No. 3 (The Divine Poem)—a melody that rises and becomes. Scriabin valued creation as a kind of inner divinity: the idea that we can build something real and lasting by choosing to create, not just consume. The sun emblem reflects the same idea: a bright beginning that grows into a legacy that makes the world more special.

Scriabin is mentioned as artistic inspiration only—no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is a tropical food tree in the Moraceae family, closely related to figs and jackfruit. It has a deep cultural history and produces seeds that can be dried, roasted, and milled into a satisfying ingredient used in both traditional and modern cooking.

Because of its wide range, Alicastrum is known by many regional names, including ramón, ojoche, capomo, and other local variants used throughout Mesoamerica and nearby regions—different names for the same remarkable tree. Today, it is increasingly recognized as a resilient food tree with real potential for future kitchens.

The seeds are the heart of the story: once roasted, they develop a comforting toasted character that translates beautifully into flour—making Alicastrum both culturally significant and genuinely practical.

Try MayaNut™ roasted seed flour

MayaNut™ flour is made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Through careful roasting, it develops a warm, satisfying depth with a gentle cocoa-like note and a naturally rich, toasted aroma.

Smooth and versatile, it can be blended into smoothies and açaí bowls, whisked into warm drinks, or used in baking and everyday cooking to add flavor and a unique roasted character—without overpowering other ingredients.

The MayaNut Company is building clear standards for growing, roasting, and milling so MayaNut™ stays consistent in taste, aroma, and performance across recipes.
