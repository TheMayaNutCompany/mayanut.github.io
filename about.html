MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

About The MayaNut Company

Building a long-term, tree-based food and reforestation industry around Brosimum alicastrum.

Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by August Kokus to establish MayaNut in Florida as a long-term agricultural tree crop and future food ingredient. The work emphasizes seed-grown diversity, long-horizon selection, and building a foundation for future cultivation and products.

Why MayaNut

Brosimum alicastrum is in the Moraceae family (relatives include figs and jackfruit). Its fruit is fig-like, but the seed is the primary food focus.

Historically, communities roasted seeds into drinks and milled them into flour—supporting the idea of MayaNut as a durable, tree-based staple.

Building an industry

Developing a new crop requires time, selection, and infrastructure. Domestication, breeding, and propagation are central to developing productive lines suited for Florida’s conditions.

As parts of Florida agriculture evolve, perennial tree crops like MayaNut can contribute to resilient systems that combine food value with long-term canopy benefits.

Mission

The mission is to advance cultivation and awareness of Brosimum alicastrum in the United States, starting in Florida, while contributing to research, selection, and responsible expansion in other suitable regions.

The current phase focuses on building planting stock, documenting performance, and preparing for Florida’s first dedicated MayaNut farm.
