About The MayaNut Company

Founder

The MayaNut Company was founded in 2025 by botanist August Kokus. His goal was to create something that could outlast him and represent his memory for centuries. Whatever it was, it had to be unique.

His fascination with plants and interest in business proved to be a winning combination. MayaNut offered something rare: a tree with real nutritional value, deep cultural history, and almost no modern development in the United States.

Why MayaNut

Brosimum alicastrum belongs to the Moraceae family alongside figs and jackfruit. Its fruit resembles a small fig, but its seed holds far greater potential as a food.

For centuries, people roasted the seeds into drinks, ground them into flour, and relied on the tree as a resilient food source during hardship.

Building an industry

Building an industry from the ground up — literally — takes time. Selective breeding, domestication, and long-term planning are essential to developing productive MayaNut cultivars.

Western-central Florida offers a rare opportunity, where a declining citrus industry can be replaced by higher-yielding, more resilient crops like MayaNut.

Mission

Our goal is simple: initiate and sustain the cultivation of Brosimum alicastrum in the United States, and eventually across other tropical regions of the world.

By 2030, we aim to establish at least one acre of land planted with over 200 seed-grown MayaNut trees, many of which are already outgrowing their pots.
