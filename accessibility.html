Information We Collect
We may collect information in these categories:
  • Contact details you send us (name, email, message content).
  • Basic device & usage data (pages visited, time on page, referrer).

How We Use It
We use information to:
  • Respond to questions, requests, and customer support.
  • Improve site content, load time, and usability (including SEO performance).
  • Understand what pages help people learn about MayaNut™ flour and roasted seed powder.
Cookies & Analytics
Cookies help the site function and (if enabled) help measure traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser. Some features may not work as intended if cookies are blocked.
  • Essential cookies: required for basic functionality.
  • Analytics: helps us understand which pages are useful (optional when applicable).
Sharing & Security
We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information only when needed to operate the site (for example: hosting, email delivery, or analytics providers).
  • We limit access to information to what is needed to run the business.
  • We use reasonable safeguards, but no method is 100% secure.
Your Choices
You can:
  • Request deletion or correction of information you sent via contact.
  • Opt out of non-essential cookies via browser settings.
  • Email us for privacy questions at info@mayanut.com.
Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy as the site grows. When updates are made, we’ll revise this page. For questions, contact info@mayanut.com.
