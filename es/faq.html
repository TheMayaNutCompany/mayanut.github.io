About MayaNut™

Core questions about what MayaNut™ is, which tree it comes from, and how it is prepared for modern use.

What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ Flour is a roasted, finely milled powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. It brings warm roasted depth with smooth cocoa-like notes to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, oatmeal, and everyday cooking.

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Alicastrum is the food tree Brosimum alicastrum. Its seeds can be dried, roasted, and milled into a fine powder used in drinks, baking, bowls, and other recipes.

Is MayaNut™ a nut?

MayaNut™ is made from a seed, not from a culinary nut. If you have allergies or strict dietary requirements, always rely on the allergen statement and facility handling notes shown on the final packaging.

How is MayaNut™ made?

The seeds are dried, roasted to develop flavor, and then milled into a smooth powder. Roast level and grind may vary slightly by batch and producer.

Is MayaNut™ connected to long-standing Mesoamerican use?

Yes. The Alicastrum tree has deep historical use in Mesoamerica, and MayaNut™ presents that traditional ingredient in a simple roasted powder format for modern kitchens.

Does MayaNut™ contain coffee or chocolate?

No. MayaNut™ does not contain coffee or chocolate by default. Its cocoa-like character comes naturally from roasting.

Ingredients + Names

Basic information about the ingredient itself and the most common regional names linked to Alicastrum.

What are the ingredients in MayaNut™?

The ingredient is 100 percent roasted Alicastrum seed powder (Brosimum alicastrum).

What is Brosimum alicastrum?

Brosimum alicastrum is the botanical name of the Alicastrum tree.

What else is Alicastrum called?

Regional names include ramón or ramon, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro or guaimaro, oox or ox, ujuxte, and masica.

Is MayaNut™ connected to names like ramón, ojoche, and capomo?

Yes. Those are regional names associated with the same Alicastrum tree and its seeds.

Does MayaNut™ contain sweeteners or additives?

The core ingredient is roasted Alicastrum seed powder. Final packaging details should always be confirmed on the product label when available.

Taste

What MayaNut™ tastes like, how strong it is, and which flavors pair especially well with it.

What does MayaNut™ taste like?

It has a warm roasted character with smooth cocoa-like notes. It does not contain cocoa by default; that cocoa-like character comes from roasting.

Is it bitter?

Usually not. If you use a large amount at once, the roast can feel more intense, so it helps to start smaller and adjust to taste.

Is MayaNut™ sweet on its own?

It is normally unsweetened. The flavor tends to be more warm, roasted, and softly cocoa-like than sugary.

How strong is the roasted flavor?

The roasted character can feel milder or bolder depending on how much you use, what you blend it with, and the roast profile of the batch.

What flavors pair well with MayaNut™?

Cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, banana, berries, and many warm spices pair especially well. It also works well with milk and plant-based alternatives.

How to Use It

Practical ways to use MayaNut™ in drinks, bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

How much should I use in smoothies or bowls?

Start with 1 to 2 tablespoons per smoothie or bowl and adjust to taste. It blends well into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, and baking, and pairs especially well with cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, and fruit.

Can I bake with MayaNut™?

Yes. Start with a smaller amount to add roasted depth, then increase as you learn the texture and flavor you prefer. Liquids and structure may need adjustment at larger amounts.

Does it dissolve in liquids?

It disperses best when blended or whisked well. For the smoothest texture in cold drinks, blending is usually best.

Can I use it in hot drinks?

Yes. Many people mix it into hot drinks as a naturally caffeine-free coffee alternative or simply to add roasted depth.

Can I mix it into oatmeal or yogurt?

Yes. It works well in oatmeal, yogurt-style bowls, and other daily recipes where you want more roasted depth.

Can I blend MayaNut™ into protein shakes?

Yes. It can blend nicely into protein shakes, especially with cocoa, banana, cinnamon, vanilla, or plant-based milks.

Diet + Nutrition

Common questions about caffeine, gluten, vegan use, paleo-friendly fit, and general nutrition.

Is MayaNut™ naturally caffeine-free?

Yes. MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free. Any caffeine would come from other ingredients you add, such as coffee or tea.

Is MayaNut™ gluten-free and vegan?

MayaNut™ is naturally gluten-free and plant-based. If you have strict requirements, confirm final facility handling statements and packaging details.

Is MayaNut™ paleo-friendly?

Many people consider roasted seed powders to be paleo-friendly, though exact diet definitions can vary.

Is MayaNut™ plant-based?

Yes. It comes from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree and is plant-based by nature.

What nutrients does MayaNut™ generally contain?

Roasted Alicastrum seed powder is often associated with dietary fiber, plant protein, and minerals such as calcium, potassium, iron, and zinc. Exact nutrition depends on the final product and laboratory testing.

Storage

How to store MayaNut™ and help keep it fresh after opening.

How should I store MayaNut™?

Store it sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keeping it airtight helps preserve aroma and freshness.

Does it need refrigeration?

Usually not, but refrigeration can help in hot or humid climates if the package stays tightly sealed.

How long does it last after opening?

Follow the best-by date shown on the bag. Keeping it sealed, cool, and dry helps maintain freshness after opening.

Can heat or humidity affect it?

Yes. Excess heat, moisture, and repeated exposure to air can reduce freshness and aroma over time.

Should I keep it airtight?

Yes. Resealing the pouch well or keeping the powder in an airtight container is the best way to preserve quality.
