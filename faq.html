About MayaNut™ Flour

What is MayaNut™ flour?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), used like a flavorful flour for smoothies and baking.

What is Alicastrum?

Alicastrum is the food tree Brosimum alicastrum. Its seeds can be roasted and milled into MayaNut™ flour.

Where does MayaNut™ come from?

MayaNut™ flour comes from roasted seeds of Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum). Packaging will list exact sourcing details when available.

Is MayaNut™ a nut or a seed?

MayaNut™ flour is made from a seed, not a culinary nut. Always check allergen and handling statements for your needs.

Why is MayaNut™ called MayaNut?

MayaNut™ is the name we use for our Alicastrum seed flour product. It helps people recognize the ingredient and how it’s used.

How long has MayaNut™ been used traditionally?

Alicastrum seeds have a long history of traditional use in parts of the Americas. Modern product forms and labeling vary by producer.

What makes MayaNut™ flour unique?

It’s a roasted seed flour with a warm, toasted flavor and a gentle cocoa-like note. It’s also naturally gluten-free and vegan.

Is MayaNut™ flour a superfood?

Some people call it a superfood, but we focus on what it is and how it tastes. Nutrition details depend on serving size and labeling.

What is roasted seed flour?

Roasted seed flour is made by roasting edible seeds and milling them into a fine powder. Roasting builds flavor and aroma.

How is MayaNut™ flour made?

The seeds are roasted and milled into a flour-like powder. Exact process details can vary by batch and packaging specs.

Is MayaNut™ flour minimally processed?

It is designed to be a simple roasted-and-milled ingredient. Always confirm the ingredient list on the label.

What part of the Alicastrum tree is used?

MayaNut™ flour is made from Alicastrum seeds. The edible seed is roasted and ground.

Is MayaNut™ flour naturally caffeine-free?

MayaNut™ flour is intended to be naturally caffeine-free. Any caffeine would come from what you mix it with.

Is MayaNut™ flour naturally sweet?

It’s typically unsweetened with a warm roasted profile. Perceived sweetness depends on the recipe.

Does MayaNut™ contain chocolate?

No—MayaNut™ does not contain chocolate by default. The cocoa-like note comes from roasting.

Storage + Ingredients

How should I store MayaNut™ flour?

Store it sealed in a cool, dry place. An airtight container helps preserve aroma and freshness.

Does MayaNut™ need refrigeration?

Not required, but refrigeration can help in hot or humid climates if kept airtight.

How long does MayaNut™ flour last?

Shelf life depends on packaging and storage. Follow the best-by date on your bag.

Can MayaNut™ flour go rancid?

Like many plant-based flours, it can lose freshness over time. Store it cool, dry, and airtight.

Does MayaNut™ clump over time?

Clumping can happen if moisture gets in. Keep it sealed and whisk or sift if needed.

What is the shelf life of MayaNut™?

Shelf life will be printed on the package. Proper storage helps you get the full freshness window.

Can I freeze MayaNut™ flour?

Freezing can help preserve freshness if the container is airtight. Let it come to room temp before opening to reduce condensation.

Does MayaNut™ stay fresh after opening?

Yes, if stored airtight and dry. Reseal promptly after each use.

What does fresh MayaNut™ smell like?

Fresh MayaNut™ typically smells warm, toasted, and lightly cocoa-like. Aroma varies with roast level.

How do I know if MayaNut™ has expired?

Use the best-by date plus sensory checks. If it smells stale, overly musty, or off, replace it.
