About MayaNut™

What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a roasted seed powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. Naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, it adds warm roasted depth with gentle cocoa-like notes to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking. Naturally caffeine-free and gluten-free. Versatile and delicious—explore our recipes →

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Alicastrum is the food tree Brosimum alicastrum. Its seeds can be roasted and milled into a fine powder used in drinks and recipes.

Is MayaNut™ a nut?

MayaNut™ is made from a seed (not a culinary nut). If you have allergies, always rely on the package allergen statement and facility handling notes.

How is MayaNut™ made?

The seeds are roasted for flavor and then milled into a smooth powder. Roast level and grind can vary by batch and producer.

Diet + Allergens

Is MayaNut™ gluten-free?

MayaNut™ is naturally gluten-free. If you have strict needs, confirm facility handling statements on the label.

Is MayaNut™ vegan?

Yes—MayaNut™ is plant-based and vegan.

Is MayaNut™ naturally caffeine-free?

Yes. MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free. Any caffeine would come from what you mix it with.

Is MayaNut™ paleo-friendly?

Many people consider roasted seed powders paleo-friendly, but diet definitions vary.

Taste + Use

What does MayaNut™ taste like?

A warm toasted flavor with gentle cocoa-like notes. It contains no cocoa by default—the cocoa-like note comes from roasting.

How much should I use?

Recommendation: add 1–2 tbsp per smoothie, bowl, or recipe. Adjust to taste.

Blend into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, and baking.
Stir into warm beverages as a coffee alternative.
Pairs well with cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, and fruit.

Does it dissolve in liquids?

It disperses best when blended or whisked. For the smoothest texture, blend into cold drinks.

Storage

How should I store MayaNut™?

Store sealed in a cool, dry place. Keep it airtight to preserve aroma and freshness.

Does it need refrigeration?

Not required, but refrigeration can help in hot or humid climates if kept airtight.

Sustainability

Why is Alicastrum considered sustainable?

Perennial food trees can be long-lived and productive over time. Sustainability depends on how and where it’s grown and managed.
