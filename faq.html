About Brosimum alicastrum

Basic questions about the tree behind MayaNut™ Flour & Powder and the names used for it across different regions.

Brosimum alicastrum is the food-tree species from which MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is made. Its seeds can be dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth ingredient used in drinks, baking, bowls, and other recipes.
The ingredient is 100 percent roasted Brosimum alicastrum seed flour and powder.
Regional names include ramón or ramon, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro or guaimaro, oox or ox, ujuxte, and masica.
