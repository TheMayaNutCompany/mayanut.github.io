FAQ

Answers to common questions about MayaNut™ flour—what it is, taste, how to use it, storage, and more.

What is MayaNut™ flour?
A roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), used like a flavorful flour for smoothies and baking.
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free and vegan?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free and vegan. Always check packaging and handling practices for your needs.
What does MayaNut™ taste like?
Warm roasted flavor with a gentle cocoa-like note—great in açaí bowls, baked goods, and everyday cooking.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking recipes to add roasted depth and richness.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour in smoothies or baking?
Blend 1–2 tablespoons into smoothies or bowls, or replace a small portion of flour in baking recipes and adjust to taste and texture.
What is the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum)?
Alicastrum is a tropical food tree whose seeds can be roasted and milled to make MayaNut™ flour.
Is MayaNut™ flour a nut?
MayaNut™ flour is made from a roasted seed, not a culinary nut. If you have severe allergies, consult your medical professional and always review packaging and facility information.
What are the ingredients in MayaNut™ flour?
MayaNut™ flour is made from roasted Alicastrum seeds. Check packaging for the most current ingredient statement and handling notes.
How is MayaNut™ flour made?
Alicastrum seeds are cleaned, roasted, and finely milled into a flour-like powder. Exact steps can vary by batch and production method.
Is MayaNut™ flour sweetened or flavored?
MayaNut™ flour is typically unsweetened and unflavored—its taste comes from the natural roasting process.
Can I bake with MayaNut™ flour?
Yes. Start by replacing a small portion of flour in a recipe, then adjust to taste and texture. It’s often used to add roasted depth.
Can MayaNut™ flour be used in savory recipes?
Yes. It can add a toasted, roasted note to sauces, soups, marinades, and spice blends—start small and adjust.
How much MayaNut™ flour should I use per serving?
A simple starting point is 1–2 tablespoons per smoothie, bowl, or recipe. Adjust based on taste and desired richness.
Does MayaNut™ flour dissolve in liquids?
It blends best when whisked or blended. In hot drinks, stirring thoroughly helps distribute it evenly.
How do I avoid clumps when mixing MayaNut™ flour?
Use a blender for smoothies, whisk vigorously into liquids, or sift and mix with dry ingredients first for baking.
What is the best way to store MayaNut™ flour?
Keep it sealed in a cool, dry place away from heat and moisture. For long-term freshness, an airtight container is recommended.
How long does MayaNut™ flour stay fresh?
Shelf life depends on packaging and storage. Always follow the best-by date and storage guidance on your bag.
Should I refrigerate or freeze MayaNut™ flour?
If you live in a hot or humid climate, refrigeration or freezing in an airtight container can help preserve freshness. Avoid moisture exposure when opening.
Is MayaNut™ flour raw or roasted?
MayaNut™ flour is made from roasted seeds, which is where the warm, cocoa-like roasted note comes from.
Is MayaNut™ flour a good substitute for cocoa powder?
It can add a cocoa-like roasted depth, but it is not cocoa. Try blending it with cocoa in recipes to find your preferred flavor.
Can I use MayaNut™ flour in coffee or lattes?
Yes. Whisk or froth it into your drink for a roasted note—start small and increase to taste.
Does MayaNut™ flour contain caffeine?
MayaNut™ flour is not intended to be a caffeine source. Any caffeine comes from what you mix it with (like coffee or tea).
Does MayaNut™ flour contain added sugar?
MayaNut™ flour is typically unsweetened. Always check the label for the most accurate ingredient and nutrition information.
Is MayaNut™ flour paleo-friendly?
Many people consider it paleo-friendly because it is a simple roasted seed flour ingredient. Individual definitions vary.
Is MayaNut™ flour keto-friendly?
Keto fit depends on your personal macro targets. Check nutrition facts on the packaging and compare to your daily plan.
Is MayaNut™ flour suitable for kids?
Many families use roasted seed flours in everyday foods. For young children or dietary concerns, consult your pediatrician and introduce new foods gradually.
Is MayaNut™ flour allergen-free?
Allergen considerations depend on facilities and handling. Always check packaging for allergen statements and contact us if you need details.
Is MayaNut™ flour certified gluten-free?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free, but certification depends on testing and facilities. Always rely on the packaging claims for the specific product you purchase.
Where is MayaNut™ flour made?
Production and sourcing details can vary as we scale. For the most current information, check the product page and packaging or contact us directly.
