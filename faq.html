About MayaNut™

About

Core questions about what MayaNut™ is, what tree it comes from, and how it is made.

What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a roasted seed powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. Naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, it adds warm roasted depth with gentle cocoa-like notes to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Alicastrum is the food tree Brosimum alicastrum. Its seeds can be roasted and milled into a fine powder used in drinks, baking, and other recipes.

Is MayaNut™ a nut?

MayaNut™ is made from a seed rather than a culinary nut. If you have allergies or strict dietary requirements, always rely on the package allergen statement and facility handling notes.

How is MayaNut™ made?

The seeds are dried, roasted for flavor, and milled into a smooth powder. Roast level and grind can vary slightly by batch and producer.

Does MayaNut™ contain coffee or chocolate?

No. MayaNut™ contains neither coffee nor chocolate by default. The cocoa-like note comes from roasting.

Ingredients + Names

Details

Basic ingredient details and regional names connected to Alicastrum and MayaNut™.

What are the ingredients in MayaNut™?

The ingredient is 100% roasted Alicastrum seed powder (Brosimum alicastrum).

What other names does Alicastrum go by?

Regional names include ramón or ramon, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro or guaimaro, oox or ox, ujuxte, and masica.

Does MayaNut™ contain sweeteners or additives?

The core ingredient is roasted Alicastrum seed powder. Final packaging details should always be confirmed on the product label when available.

Taste

Flavor

A quick overview of how MayaNut™ tastes and what flavors work well with it.

What does MayaNut™ taste like?

It has a warm toasted flavor with gentle cocoa-like notes. It contains no cocoa by default—the cocoa-like note comes from roasting.

Is it bitter?

Usually no. If you use a large amount at once, the roast can taste stronger, so it is best to start smaller and adjust to taste.

What flavors pair well with MayaNut™?

Cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, banana, berries, and many warm spices pair especially well. It also works nicely with milk and milk alternatives.

How to Use

Use

Practical ways to use MayaNut™ in drinks, bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

How much should I use in smoothies or bowls?

Start with 1–2 tablespoons per smoothie or bowl and adjust to taste. It blends well into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, and baking, and pairs especially well with cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, and fruit.

Can I bake with MayaNut™?

Yes. Start by using a small amount to add roasted depth, then increase as you learn your preferred taste and texture. Liquids and structure can change at higher amounts.

Does it dissolve in liquids?

It disperses best when blended or whisked. For the smoothest texture in cold drinks, blending usually works best.

Can I use it in hot drinks?

Yes. Many people stir it into warm drinks as a caffeine-free coffee alternative or to add roasted depth.

Diet

Diet

Common dietary questions about caffeine, gluten, vegan use, and paleo-friendly use.

Is MayaNut™ naturally caffeine-free?

Yes. MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free. Any caffeine would come from other ingredients you add, such as coffee or tea.

Is MayaNut™ gluten-free and vegan?

MayaNut™ is naturally gluten-free and plant-based. If you have strict requirements, confirm facility handling statements and packaging details on the final label.

Is MayaNut™ paleo-friendly?

Many people consider roasted seed powders paleo-friendly, but exact diet definitions vary.

Storage

Storage

How to store MayaNut™ and help keep it fresh.

How should I store MayaNut™?

Store it sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keeping it airtight helps preserve aroma and freshness.

Does it need refrigeration?

Refrigeration is not usually required, but it can help in hot or humid climates if the package is kept airtight.

How long does it last after opening?

Follow the best-by date on the bag. Keeping it sealed, cool, and dry helps maintain freshness after opening.

More

A few extra questions that come up often.

Is MayaNut™ sweetened?

Typically it is unsweetened. Any sweetness comes from what you mix it with, such as fruit or other ingredients.
