Basics

What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a roasted seed powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. Naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, it adds warm roasted depth with gentle cocoa-like notes to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, oatmeal, and everyday cooking.

Is MayaNut™ a nut?

MayaNut™ is made from a seed. If you have severe allergies, always follow the allergen/handling statement on the package.

Is MayaNut™ naturally caffeine-free?

Yes—MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free. Any caffeine would come from what you mix it with (coffee, tea, etc.).

Taste

What does MayaNut™ taste like?

MayaNut™ has a warm toasted flavor with gentle cocoa-like notes. It’s not chocolate—the cocoa-like character comes from roasting.

What does it pair well with?

It pairs especially well with cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, fruit, and creamy bases (milk or milk alternatives).

How to Use

How much should I use?

Recommendation: add 1–2 tbsp per smoothie, bowl, or recipe. Adjust to taste.

What are the easiest ways to use it?

• Blend into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, and baking
• Stir into warm beverages as a coffee-style alternative
• Pairs well with cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, and fruit
For ideas, explore recipes.

Diet + Allergens

Is MayaNut™ gluten-free?

MayaNut™ is naturally gluten-free. If you have strict dietary needs, always check the label for facility/handling statements.

Is MayaNut™ vegan?

Yes—MayaNut™ is plant-based and vegan.

Is MayaNut™ paleo-friendly?

Many people consider roasted seed powders paleo-friendly, but diet definitions vary.

Storage

How should I store MayaNut™?

Store sealed in a cool, dry place. Keep it airtight to preserve aroma and freshness.
