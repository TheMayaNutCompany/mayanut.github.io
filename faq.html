About MayaNut™

What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a roasted seed powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. Naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, it adds warm roasted depth with gentle cocoa-like notes to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking. Versatile and delicious—explore our recipes →

What is the Alicastrum tree?

Alicastrum is the food tree Brosimum alicastrum. Its seeds can be roasted and milled into a fine powder used in drinks and recipes.

Is MayaNut™ a nut?

MayaNut™ is made from a seed (not a culinary nut). If you have allergies, always rely on the package allergen statement and facility handling notes.

How is MayaNut™ made?

The seeds are roasted for flavor and then milled into a smooth powder. Roast level and grind can vary by batch and producer.

Does MayaNut™ contain coffee or chocolate?

No. MayaNut™ contains neither coffee nor chocolate by default. The cocoa-like note comes from roasting.

Taste

What does MayaNut™ taste like?

A warm toasted flavor with gentle cocoa-like notes. It contains no cocoa by default—the cocoa-like note comes from roasting.

Is it bitter?

Typically no. If you use a lot at once, the roast can taste strong—start smaller and adjust to taste.

What flavors pair well with MayaNut™?

Cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, banana, berries, and many warm spices. It also works well with milk or milk alternatives.

How to use

How much should I use in smoothies or bowls?

Recommendation: add 1–2 tbsp per smoothie, bowl, or recipe. Adjust to taste.

Blend into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, and baking.
Stir into warm beverages as a coffee alternative.
Pairs well with cocoa, cinnamon, vanilla, and fruit.

Can I bake with MayaNut™?

Yes. Start by using a small amount to add roasted depth, then increase as you learn your preferred taste and texture. Liquids and structure can change at higher amounts.

Does it dissolve in liquids?

It disperses best when blended or whisked. For the smoothest texture in cold drinks, blend.

Diet

Is MayaNut™ naturally caffeine-free?

Yes. Any caffeine would come from other ingredients you add (like coffee or tea).

Is MayaNut™ gluten-free and vegan?

MayaNut™ is naturally gluten-free and plant-based. If you have strict requirements, confirm facility handling statements on the label.

Is MayaNut™ paleo-friendly?

Many people consider roasted seed powders paleo-friendly, but diet definitions vary.

Storage

How should I store MayaNut™?

Store sealed in a cool, dry place. Keep it airtight to preserve aroma and freshness.

Does it need refrigeration?

Not required, but refrigeration can help in hot or humid climates if kept airtight.

How long does it last after opening?

Follow the best-by date on your bag. Keeping it sealed, cool, and dry helps maintain freshness.

More

Is MayaNut™ sweetened?

Typically it’s unsweetened. Any sweetness comes from what you mix it with (fruit, honey, etc.).

Can kids use MayaNut™?

Many families use roasted seed powders, but individual needs vary. Introduce new foods carefully and consult a clinician if needed.

Can I use MayaNut™ daily?

Many people use it regularly in small amounts. Your ideal amount depends on your preferences and diet.
