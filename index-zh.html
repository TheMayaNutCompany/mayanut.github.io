Common questions
What is MayaNut?
MayaNut is the roasted edible seed of Brosimum alicastrum (also called ramón, ojoche, or breadnut). It can be ground into a powder for drinks or used like a flour ingredient.
Why cultivate MayaNut in the United States?
Because it’s a perennial tree crop with potential for resilient food production, agroforestry, and canopy restoration in warm climates.
What does MayaNut taste like?
When roasted, many people describe it as nutty and toasty — a “coffee/cacao-like” vibe without caffeine. Flavor depends on roast level and genetics.
How do you use MayaNut powder?
Mix it into warm water or milk for a drink, blend into smoothies, or bake it into recipes as a supporting ingredient.
Where does Brosimum alicastrum naturally grow?
It’s native to parts of Central America, northern South America, and the Caribbean, generally in tropical and subtropical regions.
Can it grow in Florida?
In the warmest areas, yes — but performance depends on microclimate, soil, and cold events. Trialing is key.
