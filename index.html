MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum
Establishing MayaNut
as a long-term food crop
for South Florida

Over 200 seed-grown MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum) trees are currently in cultivation. Founded in 2025, The MayaNut Company is building planting stock, selecting strong performers, and preparing for future products based on this resilient tropical food tree.

The MayaNut Company is actively working to establish Brosimum alicastrum as a long-term agricultural food staple. August Kokus is developing this species for agricultural and scientific purposes. He sees the majestic beauty of the MayaNut, and hopes others will too.
