Introducing. South Florida to MayaNut.

Over 200 seed-grown MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum) trees are currently in cultivation. Founded in 2025, we are in the early stages of developing MayaNut as a long-term agricultural crop in Florida, United States, emphasizing seed-grown populations, selection, and domestication. Brosimum alicastrum has been traditionally valued in Central America for its edible tree-seed. As the project progresses, we are exploring pathways toward establishing a first MayaNut farm in South Florida, while the current population remains maintained in cultivation as long-term land options are evaluated.
