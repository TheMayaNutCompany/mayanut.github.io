What Is MayaNut?
Aromatic & Delicious
Introducing MayaNut to You

MayaNut (Maya nut) is a nutritious seed used by the ancient Mayans for centuries. It comes from a tropical tree in the jackfruit family. The seeds are ground to a powder; the powder is loved in Mexico and used to make tortillas, but can also be used like cinnamon in baked goods, drinks, acai bowls, and so much more! America deserves MayaNut, and The MayaNut Company is going to give it to them!

A Little About Us
How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, growing oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and more. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company—focused on cultivating MayaNut, continuing a Florida farming heritage.

Why We're Nuts for MayaNut

MayaNut is a productive, resilient tree overlooked by American agriculture. Since 2025, August Kokus and The MayaNut Company have been working to establish Florida’s first MayaNut farm. It is our duty and privilege to bring something new and unique into the world—something that wasn’t there before.
