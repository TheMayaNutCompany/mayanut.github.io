Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

MayaNut (Maya nut) is the seed of the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. It was used for centuries by the Maya. When roasted and ground, MayaNut becomes a rich, flavorful powder that can be enjoyed in a wide variety of foods and beverages—alongside its many nutritional benefits.

A Little About Us

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, growing oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and more. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company—focused on cultivating MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum) in Florida.

To continue our family’s Florida farming heritage, August Kokus is establishing Florida’s first MayaNut farm while openly sharing observations and cultivation research. Our mission is to develop MayaNut as a sustainable, long-term crop so others can follow, learn, and build something lasting for generations.
