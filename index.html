What is MayaNut™ Powder?

Roasted MayaNut™ Powder for Drinks, Bowls, and Recipes

MayaNut™ Powder is a caffeine-free roasted seed powder made from the Maya nut tree, Brosimum alicastrum, also known as ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, ujuxte, masica, and breadnut.

Its smooth cocoa-like roasted flavor makes it easy to enjoy by the spoonful in warm drinks, smoothies, bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, baking, and everyday recipes.

Learn About the Maya Nut Tree

MayaNut™ Powder begins with Brosimum alicastrum, the Maya nut tree, a tropical fruit tree native from Mexico to Central America and the Caribbean. Fresh fruit surrounds edible seeds that can be roasted and ground into a rich, caffeine-free powder with natural cocoa-like depth.

Known across Latin America by names such as ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, ujuxte, and masica, this species connects food, rainforest trees, traditional use, and future agriculture.
