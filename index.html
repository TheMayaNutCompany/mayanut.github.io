What is MayaNut™?

Delicious SuperfoodSeed Powder

Enjoy MayaNut™ Powder, a caffeine-free roasted powder made from the seeds of Brosimum alicastrum, also known as the Maya nut tree. Its smooth, cocoa-like flavor adds warm roasted depth to drinks, smoothies, bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, recipes, and more.

Start with a spoonful. Whisk into warm drinks, blend into smoothies, or sprinkle and stir into bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, and recipes for roasted flavor and depth.
