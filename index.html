MayaNut is Tasty A Forgotten Food

MayaNut (maya nut) is a seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. Used for centuries by the Maya, it becomes a rich, flavorful powder when roasted and ground—enjoyed in drinks, baking, and everyday recipes, alongside its many nutritional benefits.

A Little About Us
How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, growing oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and more. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating MayaNut, following in his family's farming footsteps.

Why We're Nuts for MayaNut

MayaNut is a productive, resilient tree overlooked by American agriculture. Since 2025, August Kokus and The MayaNut Company have been working to establish Florida's first MayaNut farm. It is our duty and privilege to bring something new and unique into the world; something that wasn't there before.
