What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It’s not a nut—just a delicious, cocoa-like ingredient that fits naturally into smoothies, baking, and everyday recipes.

How to use it

Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, then adjust to taste. For baking, add MayaNut™ flour for roasted depth and a richer, more satisfying flavor.
