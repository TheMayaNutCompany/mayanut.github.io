What is MayaNut™ Powder?

Delicious PremiumSuperfood

MayaNut™ Powder is a new-to-market roasted product made from the seeds of the Maya nut tree (Brosimum alicastrum), also known as ramón or breadnut, with regional names including ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica. Its bold, cocoa-like flavor makes it perfect by the spoonful in drinks, smoothies, bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, recipes, and more.

Farm Fresh

Our MayaNut™ Powder is made from carefully sourced Guatemalan seeds of Brosimum alicastrum, the Maya nut tree. Fresh fruit is harvested at the source, and the seeds inside are selected for their rich roasted flavor, smooth texture, and natural cocoa-like depth.
