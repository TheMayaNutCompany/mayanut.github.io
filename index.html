MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

The future of food grows on a forgotten tree.

MayaNut is the roasted seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a long-lived tropical canopy tree. The MayaNut Company, Inc. is a focused project in South Florida working to turn hundreds of seed-grown trees into a future source of MayaNut powder and reforestation plantings.

Over 200 seed-grown trees already in the ground – this phase is about genetics, plantings, and learning, not yet about selling.
Future MayaNut Powder & Drinks

When plantations mature, the first real offerings are expected to be MayaNut powder and powder-based drinks.
