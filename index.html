Aromatic & Delicious. Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

MayaNut is an ancient, elusive seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. For centuries, the Mayan people used this seed as food. We will soon offer it for sale. Discover the soothing flavor of MayaNut, a chocolate and coffee tasting powder used in foods, drinks, and future products. Founded in 2025, we are growing MayaNut as a long-term agricultural crop in Florida, United States, prioritizing the development of Brosimum alicastrum farms to strengthen South Florida agriculture and create a new industry from the ground up.

ABOUT
August Kokus

August Kokus develops Brosimum alicastrum for both agricultural and scientific aims. The MayaNut Company actively works to establish this species as a long-term food crop—by growing seed-grown trees, testing cultivation approaches, and documenting what we learn. By taking on this work now, we can create something that very few people have attempted, and help introduce MayaNut to a wider audience.
