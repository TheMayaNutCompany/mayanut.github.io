MayaNut is the roasted tree-seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical fruit tree in the jackfruit family that helped feed ancient Mayan communities – a true superfood almost unknown to the modern world until TODAY!

Over 200 seed-grown trees already potted up – this phase is about domestication, selecting the best quality trees for sustainable agriculture, and education. Our store is currently in development.
