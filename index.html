Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

A Little About Us

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, growing oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and more. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating MayaNut, following in his family's farming footsteps.

LEARN MORE

To continue our family’s Florida farming heritage, August Kokus is establishing Florida’s first MayaNut farm while openly sharing research and data on Brosimum alicastrum. Our mission is to develop MayaNut as a sustainable, long-term crop so others can follow, learn, and take part in building a lasting legacy for generations to come.
