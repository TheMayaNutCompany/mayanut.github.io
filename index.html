A Little About Us
How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Bubba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, cultivating oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Bubba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating Brosimum alicastrum for the production of MayaNut flour—continuing his family’s Florida farming legacy.

Why We Love MayaNut & So Should You

MayaNut™ has a warm and roasted flavor, making foods richer and more filling, and turns light meals into something that actually satisfies—perfect for açaí bowls, smoothies, baked goods, and everyday foods. Whatever kind of food you like, MayaNut™ makes it better.
