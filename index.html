MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

The future of food grows on a forgotten tree Rediscovered

MayaNut (Maya Nut) is the roasted tree-seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical species in the jackfruit family with deep ecological value and long-term potential for agroforestry and perennial food systems.

Over 200 seed-grown trees already potted up — this phase is about domestication, selecting the best quality trees for sustainable agriculture, and education. Our store is currently in development.
