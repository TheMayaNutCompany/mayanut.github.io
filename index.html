What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a roasted seed powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. Naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, it adds warm roasted depth with gentle cocoa-like notes to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

How It All Started…

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, standing alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company—working with oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company to bring Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) into modern kitchens through MayaNut™ roasted seed powder, and to support long-term cultivation standards for the future.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™?
A roasted seed powder made from the seeds of Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum).
Is MayaNut™ caffeine-free?
Yes—MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free.
Is MayaNut™ gluten-free and vegan?
Naturally gluten-free and vegan. Check handling practices for your needs.
What does it taste like?
Warm roasted depth with gentle cocoa-like notes.
How do you use it?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking.
Is it a nut or a seed?
It’s made from seeds of the Alicastrum tree (not tree nuts).
What are other names for Alicastrum?
Common names include ramón/ramon, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro/guaimaro, and oox/ox.
