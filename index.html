A Little About Us

How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, harvesting oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Bubba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum) for the production of MayaNut™ flour—continuing his family’s Florida farming legacy.

We Love MayaNut™!

Made from the seed of the rediscovered Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), MayaNut™ seed flour is truly delicious. Its warm, roasted flavor makes foods taste richer and more filling—turning simple meals into something you’ll really enjoy. Great for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, or everyday cooking, MayaNut™ is an easy pantry staple you’ll reach for week after week, all year long.
