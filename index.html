Brosimum alicastrum, known as MayaNut, is a resilient tropical fruit tree historically cultivated throughout Mesoamerica and now being reintroduced through modern Florida agriculture.

A Little About Us

Founded in 2025 by August Kokus, The MayaNut Company builds on a multi-generation family legacy of fruit cultivation in Florida. Inspired by early citrus pioneers, we are introducing MayaNut as a new agricultural crop with deep historical roots.

Today our focus is seed-grown MayaNut trees, genetic selection, and climate-ready farming systems. Our long-term vision is a scalable tropical crop supporting food security, sustainable agriculture, and ecological resilience.
