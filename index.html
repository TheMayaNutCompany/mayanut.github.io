What is MayaNut

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It adds a warm, roasted depth with a gentle cocoa-like note to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

How It All Started

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company—harvesting oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, August Kokus founded The MayaNut Company, focused on cultivating Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) to produce MayaNut™ flour.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™ flour?
A roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), used for smoothies and baking.
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free and vegan?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free and vegan. Check packaging/handling for your needs.
What does MayaNut™ taste like?
Warm roasted flavor with a gentle cocoa-like note—great in sweet or savory recipes.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking for roasted depth.
Is MayaNut™ a nut?
No—MayaNut™ comes from the seed of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum).
Does MayaNut™ contain caffeine?
No. MayaNut™ flour is naturally caffeine-free.
