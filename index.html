What is MayaNut™ Flour & Powder?

Bold, RoastedSuperfood

MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is a roasted, finely milled flour and powder made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds. It can be added to açaí bowls, smoothies, oatmeal, pancakes, cookies, muffins, and baked goods, or prepared in warm drinks as a tea-style drink, coffee substitute, or cocoa substitute.

Rooted in a long Mesoamerican history and made from Brosimum alicastrum, MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is built to feel distinctive, versatile, nutrient-dense, and easy to use from the first scoop onward. Maya nut may be used more broadly in reference to the botanical source and common-name context around Brosimum alicastrum, while MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is The MayaNut Company's branded product.

Brosimum alicastrum

Brosimum alicastrum is a tropical species with a deep food history in Mesoamerica, a mysterious presence across regional traditions, and a strong future as the botanical source behind MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. Regional names include ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica.
