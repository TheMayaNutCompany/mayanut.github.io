What Is MayaNut?

MayaNut is an ancient and elusive seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. A comforting roasted powder with notes of chocolate and coffee, a forgotten food from the ancient world, rediscovered just for you.

A Little About Us

In the 1920s, Herbert “Bubba” Humphreys (B-U-B-A) helped build our family’s Florida growing legacy through citrus and pineapple. Today, we honor that same farming spirit by cultivating a new crop—MayaNut—built for the future of tropical agriculture.

We’re developing MayaNut products while openly sharing research and farm progress. From nursery trials to field planting, we document performance, taste, and processing so others can follow—and build with us as MayaNut grows.
