Aromatic & Delicious. Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

MayaNut is an ancient, elusive seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. For centuries, the Mayan people used this seed as food, but it has only recently been rediscovered—soon to be offered for sale. Discover a soothing, chocolate-and-coffee tasting roasted seed powder that can be used in foods, drinks, and future products. Founded in 2025, we are in the early stages of growing MayaNut as a long-term agricultural crop in Florida, United States, developing farms to strengthen South Florida agriculture and build a new industry from the ground up.

A little about us

Founded in 2025 by August Kokus, The MayaNut Company stands on a multi-generation family tradition of fruit cultivation in the United States. August’s great-grandfather, Herbert “Bubba” Humphries, was an early pioneer of Florida citrus in the 1920s. Building on that history, the company is focused on establishing MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum) as a resilient, long-term agricultural food source in Florida—doing something new and uniquely ambitious that few have attempted. ABOUT

August Kokus is working in South Florida to develop Brosimum alicastrum for both agricultural and scientific aims. As the citrus industry continues to decline, Florida must look toward new long-term perennial crops to support its agricultural future. The MayaNut Company exists to introduce people to a resilient, long-overlooked food tree—while helping ensure the preservation and practical cultivation of this species for future generations.
