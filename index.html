Aromatic & Delicious. Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

MayaNut is an ancient, elusive seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. For centuries, the Mayan people used this seed as food. We will soon offer it for sale. Discover the soothing flavor of MayaNut, a chocolate and coffee tasting powder used in foods, drinks, and future products. Founded in 2025, we are growing MayaNut as a long-term agricultural crop in Florida, United States, prioritizing the development of Brosimum alicastrum farms to strengthen South Florida agriculture and create a new industry from the ground up.

A little about us

Founded in 2025 by August Kokus, The MayaNut Company builds on a multi-generation family tradition of fruit cultivation in the United States. August’s great-grandfather, Herbert “Bubba” Humphries, helped pioneer Florida citrus in the early 1920s. That same long-term farming mindset guides the company’s mission: establish Brosimum alicastrum as a resilient, long-term agricultural food source. About

August Kokus is developing Brosimum alicastrum for both agricultural and scientific aims in South Florida. As citrus continues to decline, Florida needs new long-term perennial crops to support its agricultural future. By growing these trees, we can do something new and unique that nobody else thought to do—while helping preserve the species for future generations. Research
