A Little About Us

The MayaNut Company is building a South Florida farm focused on Brosimum alicastrum. We grow seed-grown trees, evaluate real-world performance, and lay the groundwork for a new tropical crop in the United States.

We are developing future-ready MayaNut products while openly sharing research and farm progress. From nursery trials to field planting, each step is documented so others can follow the journey as it unfolds.
