What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a delicious powder made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Valued for centuries in Mesoamerica, MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, adding a warm roasted depth and bold cocoa-like flavor to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, oatmeal, hot drinks, and everyday cooking.

We are currently developing and finalizing our product, packaging, and launch details. Thank you for your patience as we continue building MayaNut™ as a polished, consistent, and enduring ingredient for modern kitchens.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™?

A roasted seed powder made from the seeds of Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum).

Is MayaNut™ caffeine-free?

Yes—MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free.

How do you use it?

Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies, bowls, oatmeal, or baking.

Is it a nut or a seed?

It is made from seeds of the Alicastrum tree, not tree nuts.

The Alicastrum Tree

MayaNut™ comes from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species with deep historical use in Mesoamerica. Regional names include ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica. MayaNut™ presents this traditional ingredient in a simple roasted powder format designed for modern everyday use.
