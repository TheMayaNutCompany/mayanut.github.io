MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

The future of food grows on a forgotten tree Rediscovered.

MayaNut is the roasted tree-seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical fruit tree in the jackfruit family with deep ecological value and long-term potential in agroforestry and perennial food systems.

Over 200 seed-grown trees currently potted — this phase is about selection, domestication, and building a foundation for long-term cultivation.
