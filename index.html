A Little About Us

MayaNut is a tropical tree seed with a rich, roasted aroma and a naturally satisfying profile. We focus on clean, consistent presentation and clear information so visitors can explore the story and the product without clutter.

The MayaNut Company works to establish cultivation, share practical knowledge, and support long-term growth with transparent updates. As much as we do today, the goal is simple: build something real and keep it beautiful.
