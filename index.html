What Is MayaNut?

🟢 MayaNut is an elusive seed from the ancient tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. Enjoy splendid tropical notes of coffee and chocolate in this new ground-seed powder. Longing for the revitalization of a forgotten food lost to time, The MayaNut Company works South Florida to establish Florida's first MayaNut farm.

A Little About Us

🟢 In the 1920s, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys helped build our family’s Florida growing legacy through citrus and pineapple. Today, we honor that same farming spirit by cultivating a new crop—MayaNut—built for the future of tropical agriculture.

🟢 Founded in 2025 by our leader August Kokus, we’re developing MayaNut products while openly sharing research and farm progress. From nursery trials to field planting, we document performance, taste, and processing so others can follow—and build with us a legacy as MayaNut grows.
