The future of food grows on a forgotten tree.

MayaNut is the roasted seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical evergreen tree native to Mesoamerica and nearby regions. Its seeds were roasted, ground, and stored by past cultures as a stable food source.

The MayaNut Company, Inc. is translating that quiet history into a modern, highly productive super-crop. We are building seed-grown trial plantations in South Florida and designing future MayaNut powder products to match.
