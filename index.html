What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It adds a warm, roasted depth with a gentle cocoa-like note to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

MayaNut™ will be available soon. We’re finalizing quality, packaging, and consistency before launch.
