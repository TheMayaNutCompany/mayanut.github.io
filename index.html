MayaNut is the roasted tree-seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical fruit tree in the jackfruit family, providing ancient Mayans with a unique superfood unknown to the modern man until TODAY!

Over 200 seed-grown trees already in the ground – this phase is about genetics, plantings, and learning, not yet about selling.
