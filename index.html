What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ Flour is a roasted, finely milled flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Valued for centuries in Mesoamerica, it delivers smooth texture, warm roasted depth, and a bold cocoa-like flavor for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, oatmeal, and everyday cooking.

We are currently developing and finalizing our product, packaging, and launch details. Thank you for your patience as we continue building MayaNut™ Flour as a polished, consistent, and enduring ingredient for modern kitchens.

Who We Are

August Kokus founded The MayaNut Company to introduce MayaNut™ as a consistent, modern pantry ingredient rooted in the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum).

We focus on quality, clarity, and long-term responsible sourcing so more people can discover MayaNut™, enjoy it often, and keep it in their kitchens for years to come.

Our goal is to present MayaNut™ in a simple, dependable format that feels at home in everyday kitchens—whether used in smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, oatmeal, or daily cooking routines.

The Alicastrum Tree

MayaNut™ comes from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species with deep historical use in Mesoamerica. Regional names include ramón or ramon, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro or guaimaro, oox or ox, ujuxte, and masica.
