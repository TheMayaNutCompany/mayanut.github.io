What is MayaNut™?

Delicious PremiumSuperfood

MayaNut™ Powder is a caffeine-free dark roast powder made from roasted seeds of the Maya nut tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Its bold, cocoa-like depth is made for drinks, smoothies, bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, and recipes.

Introducing our premium MayaNut™, ethically sourced directly from Guatemalan forests and harvested by local families who benefit from the sale of this crop. Only the highest quality Maya nuts are gathered at peak season every March and September, then roasted and finely ground to order for a smooth dark roast with rich cocoa-like depth and exceptional taste.
