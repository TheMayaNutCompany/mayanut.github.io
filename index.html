What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It adds a warm, roasted depth with a gentle cocoa-like note to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.

How It All Started…

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, standing alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, harvesting oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum) for the production of MayaNut™ flour—continuing his family’s Florida farming legacy.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™ flour?
A roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), used like a flavorful flour for smoothies and baking.
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free and vegan?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free and vegan. Always check packaging and handling practices for your needs.
What does MayaNut™ taste like?
Warm roasted flavor with a gentle cocoa-like note—great in açaí bowls, baked goods, and everyday cooking.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking recipes to add roasted depth and richness.
