What is MayaNut™ Powder?

Scrumptious, AromaticFlavor

MayaNut™ Powder is a caffeine-free dark roast made from roasted Maya nut seeds. Its cocoa-like depth is perfect for drinks, smoothies, bowls, baking, and recipes.

MayaNut™ Flour is a lighter roast milled from Maya nut seeds. Its mild flavor is made for pancakes, breads, cookies, baking, and everyday recipes.

MayaNut™ Cookies bring roasted Maya nut flavor to a familiar, shareable treat. The first varieties are coming soon and will appear in the Store when available.
