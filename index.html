What Is MayaNut?

MayaNut is an ancient seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. Enjoy splendid tropical notes of coffee and chocolate in this new seed. Longing for the revitalization of a forgotten food lost to time, The MayaNut Company is working in South Florida to establish Florida's first MayaNut farm.

A Little About Us

In the 1920s, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys helped build our family’s Florida growing legacy through citrus and pineapple. Today, we honor that same farming spirit by cultivating a new crop—MayaNut—built for the future of tropical agriculture.

We’re developing MayaNut products while openly sharing research and farm progress. From nursery trials to field planting, we document performance, taste, and processing so others can follow—and build with us as MayaNut grows.
