What is MayaNut flour?

How it all started…
In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, harvesting oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum) for the production of MayaNut™ flour—continuing his family’s Florida farming legacy.

What does MayaNut taste like?

Is MayaNut flour gluten-free and vegan?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free and vegan. Always check handling and packaging for your dietary needs.
What is the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum)?
