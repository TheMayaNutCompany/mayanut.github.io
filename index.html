How It All Started…

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company and grew Florida crops including oranges, pineapples, and watermelons. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company—focused on cultivating the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum) and developing MayaNut™ flour from its seeds.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™ flour?
A roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum), used like a flavorful flour for smoothies and baking.
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free and vegan?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free and vegan. Always check packaging and handling practices for your needs.
What does MayaNut™ taste like?
Warm roasted flavor with a gentle cocoa-like note—great in smoothies, açaí bowls, baked goods, and everyday cooking.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking recipes to add roasted depth and richness.
Is MayaNut™ a nut or a seed?
MayaNut™ is made from Alicastrum seeds, roasted and milled into flour.
