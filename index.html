What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a delicious roasted powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Valued for centuries in Mesoamerica, MayaNut™ is naturally caffeine-free and nutrient-dense, adding warm roasted depth and a rich cocoa-like flavor to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, everyday cooking, and more.

We are currently developing and finalizing our product, packaging, and launch details. Thank you for your patience as we continue building MayaNut™ as a polished, consistent, and enduring ingredient for modern kitchens.

The Alicastrum Tree

MayaNut™ comes from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species with deep historical use in Mesoamerica. Regional names include ramón or ramon, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro or guaimaro, oox or ox, ujuxte, and masica. MayaNut™ presents this traditional ingredient in a simple roasted powder format designed for modern everyday use.
