MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

The future of food grows on a forgotten tree Rediscovered

MayaNut (Maya Nut) is the roasted tree-seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical fruit tree in the jackfruit family that helped feed ancient Mayan communities — a true superfood almost unknown to the modern world until TODAY!

Over 200 seed-grown trees already potted up — this phase is about domestication, selecting the best quality trees for sustainable agriculture, and education. The store is currently in development.
