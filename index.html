MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

The future of
food grows on a
forgotten tree.

MayaNut is America's new agricultural resource, providing nutritional support and tasty memories for all to enjoy. It is the roasted seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical canopy tree emerging from a forgotten time.

Over 200 seed-grown trees already in the ground – this phase is about genetics, plantings, and learning, not yet about selling.
Future MayaNut Powder & Drinks

These products are conceptual. We are currently developing Florida's First MayaNut plantation. We expect to begin selling MayaNut powder by 2029. Thank you for your patience and support!

MayaNut Powder

Roasted seed powder – smooth, caffeine-free, and ready to mix into drinks or food.

Instant MayaNut Latte

A future drink mix for warm, creamy cups based on tree-grown MayaNut powder.

MayaNut Baking Blend

A gluten-free oriented blend for cookies, bars, and breads with a forest story.

Cold MayaNut Drink

A chilled, smooth, tree-based drink concept built on MayaNut powder and plant milk.
