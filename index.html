A Little About Us

How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, harvesting oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum) for the production of MayaNut™ flour—continuing his family’s Florida farming legacy.

We Love MayaNut™!

Made from the seed of the rediscovered Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), MayaNut™ seed flour is truly a unique product. Its warm, roasted flavor makes foods and drinks taste richer and more satisfying—turning simple meals into something you’ll really enjoy. Perfect for smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, or everyday recipes, MayaNut™ is an easy pantry staple you can enjoy week after week, any season, any time of the year.
