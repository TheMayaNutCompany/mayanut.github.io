What Is MayaNut?

Aromatic & Delicious

Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

MayaNut (maya nut) is a seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum, traditionally used by the Maya. When roasted and ground, it becomes a rich, flavorful powder for foods and beverages.

A Little About Us

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys and his father Frank Humphreys founded Kennesaw Fruit Company. In 2025, August Kokus founded The MayaNut Company to cultivate MayaNut in Florida.
