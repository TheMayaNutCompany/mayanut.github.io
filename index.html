How It All Started…

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, standing alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, harvesting oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused on Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) and the production of MayaNut™ flour in Florida.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™ flour?
A roasted seed flour made from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum).
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free and vegan?
Naturally gluten-free and vegan. Check handling practices for your needs.
What does MayaNut™ taste like?
Warm roasted flavor with a gentle cocoa-like note.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking.
Is MayaNut™ a nut or a seed?
It’s made from seeds of the Alicastrum tree (not tree nuts).
