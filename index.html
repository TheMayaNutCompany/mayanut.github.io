What is MayaNut™ Powder?

Bold, RoastedSuperfood

MayaNut™ Powder by The MayaNut Company is a darker roasted powder for warm drinks, tea-style drinks, coffee substitutes, cocoa substitutes, smoothies, oatmeal, and bowls. It is made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds. In broader botanical language, Maya nut refers to the seed and source tree, while MayaNut™ Powder is the branded product from The MayaNut Company.

Rooted in a long Mesoamerican history and made from Brosimum alicastrum, MayaNut™ Powder is a brand built to feel distinctive, versatile, nutrient-dense, and easy to use from the first scoop onward. Maya nut may be used more broadly in reference to the botanical source and common-name context around Brosimum alicastrum, while MayaNut™ Powder is The MayaNut Company’s branded product.

Brosimum alicastrum

Brosimum alicastrum is a tropical species with a deep food history in Mesoamerica, a mysterious presence across regional traditions, and a strong future as the botanical source behind MayaNut™ Powder. The broader Maya nut context belongs to this botanical and cultural background, while the branded product name remains MayaNut™ Powder. Regional names include ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica.
