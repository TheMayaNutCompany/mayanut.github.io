A Little About Us

How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Bubba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, cultivating oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and other crops. In 2025, Bubba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum) for the production of MayaNut™ flour—continuing his family’s Florida farming legacy.

We Love MayaNut™!

Used by the ancient Maya, MayaNut™ has a warm and roasted flavor, making foods richer, more filling, and turns light meals into something that actually satisfies—perfect for açaí bowls, smoothies, baked goods, and everyday foods. Made from seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), MayaNut™ brings a long-used natural ingredient into modern cuisine—and it simply tastes great.
