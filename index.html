MayaNut • Brosimum alicastrum

Establishing MayaNut
as a long-term food crop for South Florida

Over 200 seed-grown MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum) trees are currently in cultivation. Founded in 2025, we are building a foundation around seed-grown diversity, selection, and domestication—so this species can become a real, scalable crop over time.

Seed-grown diversity • Florida trials
What it is
MayaNut is the roasted tree-seed of Brosimum alicastrum, a resilient tropical food tree in Moraceae (fig relatives).
Why it matters
Perennial crops can support long-term agriculture. Our focus is to prove performance in Florida and document results clearly.
How we’re doing it
Seed-grown populations, selection, and patient domestication—then products once cultivation and processing are ready.
