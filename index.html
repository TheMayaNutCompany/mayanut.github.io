What is MayaNut™ Flour & Powder?

Bold Roasted MayaNut™ Flour & Powder

MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is a roasted, finely milled powdered ingredient, pantry spice, and nutrient-dense superfood made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds. It can be added to açaí bowls, smoothies, oatmeal, pancakes, cookies, muffins, and baked goods, or prepared in warm drinks as a tea-style drink, coffee substitute, or cocoa substitute.

Rooted in a long Mesoamerican history and made from Brosimum alicastrum, MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is built to feel distinctive, versatile, nutrient-dense, and easy to use from the first scoop onward.

Who We Are

August Kokus founded The MayaNut Company in 2025 to introduce MayaNut™ Flour & Powder as a modern pantry ingredient and build a long-term identity around Brosimum alicastrum, the species behind the product.

The company is being built around MayaNut™ Flour & Powder and broader Brosimum alicastrum awareness so more people can discover the product and connect it to the species behind it.

Brosimum alicastrum

Brosimum alicastrum is a tropical species with a deep food history in Mesoamerica, a mysterious presence across regional traditions, and a strong future as the botanical source behind MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. Regional names include ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica.
