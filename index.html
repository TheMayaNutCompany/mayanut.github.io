What is MayaNut™?

MayaNut™ is a powder made from the roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Used for centuries as a traditional food in Mesoamerica, MayaNut™ is naturally vegan, caffeine-free, and nutrient-dense, adding a bold cocoa-like flavor to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, and everyday cooking.
