What Is MayaNut?

🟢 MayaNut (maya nut) is a seed that comes from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. People have used MayaNut for thousands of years—especially the ancient Maya, from whom the tree gets its name. The seed can be roasted and ground, much like coffee or cocoa, and used to make flatbreads, cookies, and beverages.

The MayaNut Company exists to bring this lost food back into the spotlight and to support the development of America’s first MayaNut farm, so this unique food can be enjoyed again for generations to come.

A Little About Us

🟢 In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, growing oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and more. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating MayaNut.

🟢 To continue our family’s Florida farming heritage, August Kokus is establishing Florida’s first MayaNut farm while openly sharing research and data on Brosimum alicastrum. Our mission is to develop MayaNut as a sustainable, long-term crop so others can follow, learn, and take part in building a lasting legacy for generations to come.
