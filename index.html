A Little About Us

The MayaNut Company is building a South Florida farm focused on Brosimum alicastrum. We’re growing seed-grown trees, testing what performs best, and building the foundation for a new tropical crop in the U.S.

We’re developing future-ready MayaNut products—while sharing research and farm progress openly as we scale. From nursery trials to field planting, every step is documented so supporters can follow the journey in real time.
