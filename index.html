Introducing MayaNut to You

MayaNut (maya nut) is a seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. Used for centuries by the Maya, it becomes a rich, flavorful powder when roasted and ground— enjoyed in drinks, baking, and everyday recipes, alongside its many nutritional benefits.
