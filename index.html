What is MayaNut™ Flour & Powder?

Bold, RoastedSuperfood

MayaNut™ Flour is a roasted, finely milled flour for pancakes, cookies, muffins, baked goods, oatmeal, smoothies, and açaí bowls, while MayaNut™ Powder is a darker roasted powder for warm drinks, tea-style drinks, coffee substitutes, cocoa substitutes, smoothies, oatmeal, and bowls; both are made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds, and in broader botanical language, Maya nut refers to the seed and source tree while MayaNut™ Flour & Powder remains the branded product from The MayaNut Company.

Rooted in a long Mesoamerican history and made from Brosimum alicastrum, MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is a brand built to feel distinctive, versatile, nutrient-dense, and easy to use from the first scoop onward. Maya nut may be used more broadly in reference to the botanical source and common-name context around Brosimum alicastrum, while MayaNut™ Flour & Powder is The MayaNut Company's branded product.

Brosimum alicastrum

Brosimum alicastrum is a tropical species with a deep food history in Mesoamerica, a mysterious presence across regional traditions, and a strong future as the botanical source behind MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. The broader Maya nut context belongs to this botanical and cultural background, while the branded product name remains MayaNut™ Flour & Powder. Regional names include ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica.
