Introducing. South Florida to MayaNut.

Over 200 seed-grown MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum) trees are currently in cultivation.
Founded in 2025, we are in the early stages of developing MayaNut as a long-term agricultural crop in Florida, United States, with an emphasis on seed-grown populations, selection, and domestication.
Brosimum alicastrum has been traditionally valued in Central America for its edible tree-seed. These trees represent an early domestic population in a new agricultural context.

As the project develops, we are exploring pathways toward establishing a first MayaNut farm in South Florida, with the current population maintained in cultivation.
