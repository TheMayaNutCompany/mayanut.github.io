What is MayaNut™?

How It All Started…

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Harry Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company and grew Florida crops. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company—focused on developing MayaNut™ flour from Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) and building a modern roasted seed flour category.

FAQ

What is MayaNut™ flour?
MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum).
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free and vegan?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free and vegan. Always check packaging and handling practices for your needs.
What does MayaNut™ taste like?
Warm roasted flavor with a gentle cocoa-like note—great in açaí bowls, baked goods, and everyday cooking.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or mix into baking recipes to add roasted depth and richness.
Is MayaNut™ a nut?
MayaNut™ is made from Alicastrum seeds milled into roasted seed flour—not a tree nut ingredient.
When will MayaNut™ be available?
We’re finalizing inventory and packaging now. Launch updates will be posted on the site and our socials.
How should I store MayaNut™ flour?
Store sealed in a cool, dry place. For best freshness after opening, keep it tightly closed.
