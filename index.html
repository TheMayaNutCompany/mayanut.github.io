What Is MayaNut?
A Delicious, Roasted Seed Flour

MayaNut (Maya nut) is not a nut, but a naturally gluten-free, highly nutritious seed harvested from the rainforest tree Brosimum alicastrum, a member of the jackfruit family. Valued for thousands of years and deeply connected to Maya culture, MayaNut has long been used as a nourishing staple food.

Once harvested, the seeds are ground into a versatile powder used in breads, tortillas, beverages, baked goods, drinks, açaí bowls, granola, and other foods—bringing an ancient, sustainable ingredient into modern cuisine.

A Little About Us
How It All Started...

In 1923, Herbert “Buba” Humphreys, alongside his father Frank Humphreys, founded Kennesaw Fruit Company, growing oranges, pineapples, watermelons, and more. In 2025, Buba’s great-grandson, August Kokus, founded The MayaNut Company, focused exclusively on cultivating MayaNut, following in his family's farming footsteps.

Why We're Nuts for MayaNut

MayaNut is a productive, resilient tree overlooked by American agriculture. Since 2025, August Kokus and The MayaNut Company have been working to establish Florida's first MayaNut farm. It is our duty and privilege to bring something new and unique into the world; something that wasn't there before. MayaNut is our gift to the world.
