Aromatic & Delicious. Introducing MayaNut to You 🫵

MayaNut is an ancient, elusive seed from the tropical tree Brosimum alicastrum. For centuries, the Mayan people used this seed as food, but it has only recently been rediscovered—soon to be offered for sale. Discover a soothing, chocolate-and-coffee tasting roasted seed powder that can be used in foods, drinks, and future products. Founded in 2025, we are in the early stages of growing MayaNut as a long-term agricultural crop in Florida, United States, developing farms to strengthen South Florida agriculture and build a new industry from the ground up.

A little about us

Founded in 2025 by August Kokus, The MayaNut Company stands on a multi-generation family tradition of fruit cultivation in the United States. Building on that history, August Kokus is introducing a new crop—MayaNut (Brosimum alicastrum)—to modern American agriculture.

August Kokus is working in South Florida to establish Brosimum alicastrum as a new agricultural cornerstone. As the citrus industry continues to decline, Florida must look toward new long-term perennial crops to support its agricultural future. August created The MayaNut Company to introduce people to a resilient, long-overlooked food tree.

The MayaNut Company actively works to establish Brosimum alicastrum as a long-term agricultural food source. August Kokus develops this species for both agricultural and scientific aims.

By growing these trees, we can do something new and unique that nobody else thought to do. We hope you love MayaNut as much as we do.
