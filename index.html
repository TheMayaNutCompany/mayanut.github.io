What is MayaNut™?

Roasted SuperfoodSeed Powder

MayaNut™ Powder is a caffeine-free, finely milled seed powder made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds, with warm cocoa-like depth for warm drinks, smoothies, bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, and recipes.

Start with a spoonful. Whisk into warm drinks, blend into smoothies, or sprinkle and stir into bowls, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, and recipes for roasted flavor and depth.
