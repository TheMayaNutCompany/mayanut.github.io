What is MayaNut™ Flour?

MayaNut™ Flour is a roasted, finely milled flour made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds, from a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica.

Maya nut may be used more broadly in reference to the botanical source and common-name context around Brosimum alicastrum, while MayaNut™ Flour is The MayaNut Company's branded flour product.

Carefully dried and gently roasted, this Guatemalan-sourced flour delivers smooth texture and a mellow roasted profile for pancakes, muffins, cookies, brownies, breads, flatbreads, and other baking-forward uses.

MayaNut™ Flour is the baking-forward option for pancakes, muffins, cookies, brownies, breads, flatbreads, and other flour-style recipes.

Ingredients: 100% roasted, finely milled Brosimum alicastrum seed flour.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Flavor Profile

  • Warm roasted character
  • Soft, flour-style cocoa-like depth
  • Smooth, finely milled texture for baking
  • Mellow finish without bitterness

Ways to Use

  • Add to pancakes, muffins, cookies, and brownies
  • Fold into breads, flatbreads, and baked goods
  • Blend into smoothies or bowls when a flour-style texture is desired
  • Mix into oatmeal and everyday recipes
