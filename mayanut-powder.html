Introducing MayaNut™ Powder

Introducing our premium MayaNut™ Powder, ethically sourced directly from Guatemalan forests and harvested by local families who benefit from the sale of this crop. Two main harvests take place each year, in spring and summer, when the highest-quality Maya nuts are gathered, roasted, and finely ground to order for a smooth dark roast with rich cocoa-like depth and exceptional taste.

From a Revered Tree

Brosimum alicastrum is one of Mesoamerica’s most versatile food trees, yet it remains little known in many modern markets. Its seeds naturally contain dietary fiber, protein and amino acids, along with minerals including potassium, magnesium, calcium, iron, zinc, and copper. They also contain naturally occurring phenolic compounds, carotenoids, and vitamin C. Exact amounts can vary with growing region, harvest, drying, roasting, and serving size, so any specific nutrient claims for MayaNut™ Powder will be based on testing of the finished product.

MayaNut™ Powder Questions

What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is a premium, caffeine-free dark roast powder made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds.

What does MayaNut™ Powder taste like?

It has smooth cocoa-like depth, a warm roasted aroma, and a gently toasted finish. It is designed to work in both drinks and food without the bitterness or caffeine of coffee.

How do I prepare MayaNut™ Powder as a drink?

Start with a spoonful and adjust to taste. For a hot drink, whisk it into hot water, warm milk, or plant milk. For an iced drink, shake or blend it with cold milk, plant milk, or water, then pour over ice.

How else can I use MayaNut™ Powder?

Stir it into smoothies, oatmeal, or yogurt; dust it over fruit and bowls; or use it in baking and other recipes. Visit the Recipes page for more serving ideas.

What nutrients have been reported in Maya nut seed?

Published research on Brosimum alicastrum seed and flour has reported dietary fiber, protein and amino acids, minerals including potassium, magnesium, calcium, iron, zinc, and copper, and naturally occurring phenolic compounds with measured antioxidant activity. Some studies also report carotenoids and vitamin C. Exact values vary by origin, harvest, processing, roast, serving size, and finished product.

Is MayaNut™ Powder caffeine-free?

Yes. MayaNut™ Powder is caffeine-free and can be enjoyed as a roasted hot or iced drink or used in smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt, bowls, fruit, and recipes.

Is MayaNut powder the same as Maya nut flour?

Both terms can refer to ground seeds of the Maya nut tree. MayaNut™ Powder is positioned as a dark-roasted, finely ground product for drinks, toppings, bowls, and recipes.

What other names are used for Maya nut?

Brosimum alicastrum is also known regionally as ramón, breadnut, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, ujuxte, masica, ojite, ojushte, mojo, and mojote, among other names.
