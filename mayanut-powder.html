What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is a roasted, finely milled powder made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds, from a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica.

Maya nut may be used more broadly in reference to the botanical source and common-name context around Brosimum alicastrum, while MayaNut™ Powder is The MayaNut Company's branded powder product.

Carefully dried and roasted for deeper flavor, this Guatemalan-sourced powder delivers smooth texture, warm cocoa-like depth, and a bold roasted profile for smoothies, warm drinks, açaí bowls, oatmeal, coffee-style drinks, cocoa-style drinks, and other beverage-forward uses.

MayaNut™ Powder is the drink-and-bowl-forward option for smoothies, warm drinks, açaí bowls, oatmeal, cocoa-style drinks, coffee-style drinks, and everyday powder-style recipes.

Ingredients: 100% roasted, finely milled Brosimum alicastrum seed powder.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
