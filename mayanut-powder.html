From a Revered Tree

Brosimum alicastrum is one of Mesoamerica’s most versatile food trees, yet it remains little known in many modern markets. Its seeds naturally contain dietary fiber, protein and amino acids, along with minerals including potassium, magnesium, calcium, iron, zinc, and copper. They also contain naturally occurring phenolic compounds, carotenoids, and vitamin C. Exact amounts can vary with growing region, harvest, drying, roasting, and serving size, so any specific nutrient claims for MayaNut™ Powder will be based on testing of the finished product.

MayaNut™ Powder Questions

What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is a premium, caffeine-free dark roast powder made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds, the seeds of the Maya nut tree.

What does MayaNut™ Powder taste like?

MayaNut™ Powder tastes smooth, roasted, and cocoa-like, with a warm aroma and a gently toasted finish. It has roasted depth without the caffeine of coffee.

Is MayaNut™ Powder coffee?

No. MayaNut™ Powder is not coffee and is not made from coffee beans. It is a roasted seed powder made from Brosimum alicastrum seeds and can be prepared as a hot or iced drink.

Does MayaNut™ Powder contain caffeine?

No. MayaNut™ Powder is caffeine-free.

Is MayaNut™ Powder cocoa or cacao?

No. MayaNut™ Powder is not cocoa or cacao. Its smooth, cocoa-like depth describes its flavor profile; the powder is made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds.

How do I prepare MayaNut™ Powder as a drink?

Start with 1 tablespoon and adjust to taste. For a hot drink, whisk it into hot water, warm milk, or plant milk. For an iced drink, shake or blend it with cold milk, plant milk, or water, then pour over ice.

How can I use MayaNut™ Powder in food?

Stir it into smoothies, oatmeal, or yogurt; dust it over fruit and bowls; or use it in baking and other recipes. The powder adds smooth roasted depth to both sweet and everyday foods.

What is the Maya nut tree?

The Maya nut tree is Brosimum alicastrum, a tropical tree native to Mesoamerica and parts of the Caribbean and northern South America. Its seeds are harvested, roasted, and ground to make MayaNut™ Powder.

What is Brosimum alicastrum?

Brosimum alicastrum is the botanical name of the Maya nut tree. It is a member of the mulberry family, Moraceae, and its seeds have a long history of food use in Mesoamerica.

Is MayaNut powder the same as Maya nut flour?

Both terms may be used for ground Maya nut seed products, but products can differ by roast and grind. MayaNut™ Powder is a dark-roasted, finely ground product positioned for drinks, toppings, bowls, and recipes.

Is MayaNut™ Powder gluten-free and plant-based?

MayaNut™ Powder is presented by The MayaNut Company as gluten-free and plant-based. The product is made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds.

Where does MayaNut™ Powder come from?

The MayaNut Company sources Maya nuts from Guatemala, where local families harvest the seeds from forest landscapes. The seeds are roasted and finely ground into MayaNut™ Powder before the product is brought to the United States for packaging.

What other names are used for Maya nut?

Brosimum alicastrum is known by many regional names, including ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, ujuxte, masica, ojite, ojushte, mojo, and mojote. Breadnut is also used in some English-language contexts.

What nutrients have been reported in Maya nut seed?

Published research on Brosimum alicastrum seed and flour has reported dietary fiber, protein and amino acids, minerals including potassium, magnesium, calcium, iron, zinc, and copper, and naturally occurring phenolic compounds with measured antioxidant activity. Exact values vary by origin, harvest, processing, roast, serving size, and finished product; finished-product nutrition information should be based on verified testing.

How should I store MayaNut™ Powder?

Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place and close the resealable package after use. Follow any storage directions printed on the final product package.
