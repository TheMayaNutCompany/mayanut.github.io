About Our Product

Introducing our premium MayaNut™ Powder, ethically sourced directly from Guatemala and harvested by local families who benefit from the sale of this crop. Every March and September, Maya nut seeds are gathered at peak season, then roasted and finely ground to order, creating a smooth dark roast powder with rich cocoa-like depth and exceptional taste.

The MayaNut Company is dedicated to finding responsibly harvested Brosimum alicastrum seed from the forests of Mesoamerica. Through that search, we discovered a trusted source for the seeds used to create our signature MayaNut™ Powder. Enjoy this elusive pantry staple from the rainforest and bring bold, roasted depth to your food and drinks.

MayaNut™ Powder Questions

What other names are used for Maya nut?

Brosimum alicastrum and the Maya nut tree are also searched under names such as Maya nut, breadnut, bread nut, ramón, ramon, ojoche, ojite, ojushte, ujushte, ujuxte, capomo, apomo, guáimaro, guaimaro, guaymaro, masica, masico, oox, ox, oxté, iximché, mojo, mojote, uje, uji, ujo, huje, ash, ax, samaritano, Juan Diego, Nazareno, Gueltzé, Taján, Hairi, Jauri, sande, tillo, muiratinga, chara, charo amarillo, manchinga, congona, noix-pain, chokogou, and milk tree.

Can MayaNut™ Powder be searched as a healthy seed powder?

Yes. It is a plant-based roasted seed powder for people searching for healthy seed powder, caffeine-free powder, roasted seed flour, gluten-free pantry ingredients, smoothie powder, bowl toppings, baking ingredients, and cocoa-like drink powders.

What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is a premium, caffeine-free dark roast powder made from roasted Brosimum alicastrum seeds.

Is MayaNut powder the same as Maya nut flour?

Maya nut flour and MayaNut powder can both refer to ground seeds of the Maya nut tree. This product is positioned as a roasted dark roast powder for drinks, toppings, bowls, and recipes.

Is ramón powder the same as breadnut powder?

Ramón, breadnut, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, ujuxte, and masica are common names associated with Brosimum alicastrum, depending on region and language.

Is MayaNut™ Powder caffeine-free?

Yes. MayaNut™ Powder is caffeine-free and can be used as a roasted, cocoa-like powder in warm drinks, smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt, fruit, and recipes.

What is the difference between dark roast and light roast Maya nut powder?

Dark roast Maya nut powder has a deeper roasted, cocoa-like flavor. A lighter roast would be milder and could work more like a seed flour for baking and lighter recipes.

Is this an organic seed powder?

MayaNut™ Powder should only be described as organic if the product is certified and labeled that way. The page uses organic-related wording only to help shoppers compare categories without making a false certification claim.
