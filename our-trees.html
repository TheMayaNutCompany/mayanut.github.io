Our Trees

A living collection of seed-grown Brosimum alicastrum trees in South Florida, forming the foundation of Florida’s first MayaNut plantation and a longer-term Brosimeae exploration.

Current MayaNut nursery stock in South Florida – all seed-grown Brosimum alicastrum.
Field status • December 13, 2025

We currently as of December 13, 2025, have around 200 mayanut plants ready to be planted in Florida's first mayanut plantation. All seed grown, though are genetic sources are limited, The MayaNut Company plans on going on expeditions to collect all 19 species of Brosimeae. However, Brosimum alicastrum is by far the most productive and fast growing in the clade.
