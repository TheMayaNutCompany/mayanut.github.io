Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how The MayaNut Company collects, uses, and protects information when you visit mayanut.com. Our website shares information about MayaNut™ flour and seed powder, a food product made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum).

What We Collect

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name or email address when contacting us. Non-personal information such as browser type, device data, and pages visited may also be collected to improve site performance and content related to MayaNut™ flour, gluten-free seed powder, and tropical food education.

Use of Information

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, improve accessibility, enhance website functionality, and ensure accurate information about MayaNut™ products. We do not sell or trade personal data.

Cookies

Cookies may be used to understand site traffic and improve user experience. These technologies do not personally identify visitors and are used solely to optimize content related to MayaNut™ flour, seed powder, and everyday cooking.
