Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 24, 2025
Contact: info@mayanut.com

This Privacy Policy explains how The MayaNut Company, Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit mayanut.com (the “Site”). The Site is primarily an informational website. Any store elements visible today may be conceptual while full e-commerce functionality is in development.

Information we collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide: If you email us, you provide your email address and the contents of your message.
  • Basic usage data (server / hosting logs): Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time/date) for security and performance.
  • Site preferences (local storage): The Site may store accessibility preferences (for example, high contrast mode, larger text, and voiceover selection reading) in your browser using local storage so your choices persist.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and communicate with you.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To remember your accessibility preferences on your device.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Hosting and infrastructure providers that help deliver and protect the Site.
  • Legal / safety: If required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Email communication

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message content only to respond and for reasonable record-keeping. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you explicitly request it.

Analytics

The Site may use privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand traffic and improve content. If enabled, this section will be updated to describe what is used and how to opt out (where applicable).

Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention

  • Emails: Retained as needed to respond and maintain reasonable records.
  • Preference storage: Accessibility preferences remain in your browser until you clear site data/local storage.
  • Hosting logs: Retention is controlled by the hosting provider and typically kept for security and operations.

Your choices

  • You can clear local storage / site data in your browser to reset accessibility preferences.
  • You can contact us to request deletion of your email communications where feasible and legally permitted.

International visitors

If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States where our hosting and operations are based.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Store functionality is currently in development. When checkout or order processing becomes available, this policy will be updated to cover payment and order information handling.
