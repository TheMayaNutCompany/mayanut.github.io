MayaNut™ Flour

MayaNut™ Flour is a delicious roasted seed flour (often searched as MayaNut powder) made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Naturally gluten-free and fiber-rich, it has a warm, roasted, cocoa-like flavor that makes smoothies thicker, baked goods heartier, and everyday foods more satisfying.

This is not a nut. It’s a roasted seed flour from a tropical rainforest tree—an easy, nourishing ingredient you’ll want to use again and again.
