MayaNut™ flour is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It has a smooth, warming, cocoa-like flavor that makes everyday foods feel richer, heartier, and more satisfying.

Perfect in smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, beverages, and everyday cooking.

How to use it (quick ideas) + serving size

Smoothies & bowls: blend into smoothies or açaí bowls for a warm, cocoa-like roasted flavor and a more filling finish. Baking & breakfasts: stir into oatmeal, pancake batter, muffins, or banana bread for a cozy roasted note (pairs great with cinnamon and vanilla). Everyday cooking: mix into drinks, sauces, or recipes as a simple “hearty boost” ingredient—easy to add without changing your routine. Serving size: start with 1–2 tablespoons per serving and adjust to taste.
