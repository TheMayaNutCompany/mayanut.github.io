What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. After harvesting, the seeds are carefully dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth powder with a naturally warm, toasted flavor and gentle cocoa-like notes.

Naturally caffeine-free and gluten-free, MayaNut™ powder is a versatile ingredient that blends easily into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, baked goods, and warm beverages as a coffee alternative. Its mild roasted character adds depth to recipes without overpowering other ingredients.

The seeds of the Alicastrum tree are naturally nutrient-dense, providing dietary fiber, plant protein, and essential minerals including calcium, potassium, iron, and zinc, along with vitamins A, B, C, and E. Traditionally enjoyed by forest communities for generations, MayaNut™ brings this remarkable ingredient into modern kitchens in a simple and versatile form.
