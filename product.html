What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). The seeds are dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth powder with a naturally warm, toasted flavor and bold cocoa-like depth.

Ingredients: 100% roasted Alicastrum seed powder (Brosimum alicastrum).
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

For searchability and regional references, this ingredient is also called ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, and oox. A historical name you may see is Mayan breadnut.

Flavor Profile

  • Warm roasted character
  • Bold cocoa-like flavor
  • Smooth, finely milled texture
  • Mellow finish (not bitter)

Ways to Use

  • Blend into smoothies and shakes
  • Mix into açaí bowls and oatmeal
  • Add to pancakes and baked goods
  • Stir into warm drinks
