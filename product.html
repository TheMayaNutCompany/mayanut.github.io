What is MayaNut™ Powder?

MayaNut™ Powder is made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. The same ingredient may be called ramón, ojoche, capomo, guaimaro, or oox in different regions (and “mayan breadnut” / “breadnut” as a historical name).

After harvesting, the seeds are dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth powder with a naturally warm, toasted flavor and gentle cocoa-like notes. It blends easily into smoothies, açaí bowls, oatmeal, baked goods, and warm beverages as a coffee alternative.
