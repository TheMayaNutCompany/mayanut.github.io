MayaNut™ is a roasted seed powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica. Carefully dried, gently roasted, and finely milled, it delivers a smooth texture and warm roasted depth with a bold cocoa-like flavor, adding richness to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, oatmeal, and everyday cooking. Light roast stays smoother and more mellow, while dark roast is roasted longer for a deeper, bolder flavor. Explore our recipes →

What is MayaNut™ Powder?

Recipes with MayaNut

MayaNut™ Powder is made from roasted seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). The seeds are dried, roasted, and milled into a smooth powder with naturally warm, toasted depth and a bold cocoa-like flavor.

Ingredients: 100% roasted Alicastrum seed powder (Brosimum alicastrum).
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

For searchability and regional references, this ingredient is also known in different regions as ramón, ojoche, capomo, guáimaro, oox, ujuxte, and masica.

  • Mellow finish without bitterness
