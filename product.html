MayaNut™ is a roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It’s naturally gluten-free and has a warm, roasted, cocoa-like flavor that makes simple foods taste deeper and more satisfying. Think of it as a botanical food ingredient you can use like a flour—or sprinkle as a seasoning or flavoring—to add roasted richness to everyday meals.
