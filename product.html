FAQ

What does MayaNut taste like?

MayaNut™ has a warm, roasted, cocoa-like flavor that makes smoothies, baked goods, and everyday meals taste richer and more satisfying.

How do you use MayaNut flour?

Use it like a flour in baking, stir into smoothies and oatmeal, mix into drinks, or sprinkle as a seasoning/flavoring. Start with 1–2 tablespoons and adjust to taste.

Is MayaNut gluten-free?

Yes—MayaNut™ is naturally gluten-free because it’s made from roasted Alicastrum tree seeds (Brosimum alicastrum), not wheat.
