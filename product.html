MayaNut™ Powder

MayaNut™ powder is a delicious roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). Naturally gluten-free and fiber-rich, MayaNut™ has a warm, cocoa-like roasted flavor that makes smoothies thicker, baked goods heartier, and everyday foods more satisfying.

Used for centuries in Central America, MayaNut™ brings a long-used sustainable ingredient into modern American cuisine. This is not a nut—it is a roasted seed flour from a tropical rainforest tree.
