What is MayaNut™ Flour?

Recipes with MayaNut

MayaNut™ flour is a roasted, finely milled powder made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum), a tropical species long valued as a traditional food in Mesoamerica.

Carefully dried and gently roasted, this Guatemalan-sourced flour delivers a smooth texture, warm roasted depth, and a bold, cocoa-like flavor. It brings richness to smoothies, açaí bowls, baking, warm drinks, oatmeal, and everyday cooking.

Light roast offers a smoother, more mellow profile, while dark roast is roasted longer for a deeper, bolder flavor.

Ingredients: 100% roasted, finely milled Alicastrum seed flour (Brosimum alicastrum).
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Flavor Profile

  • Warm roasted character
  • Bold, cocoa-like flavor
  • Smooth, finely milled texture
  • Mellow finish without bitterness

Ways to Use

  • Blend into smoothies and shakes
  • Mix into açaí bowls and oatmeal
  • Add to pancakes and baked goods
  • Stir into warm drinks and everyday recipes
