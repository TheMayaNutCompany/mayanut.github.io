MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie
Vegan Gluten-Free 5 min
A creamy smoothie with a warm, roasted cocoa-like depth from MayaNut™ flour (often searched as MayaNut powder).
Ingredients
  • 1–2 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1–1½ cups plant milk
  • ½ tsp cinnamon (optional)
  • 1 tsp vanilla (optional)
  • Sweetener to taste (optional)
Method
  • Add everything to a blender
  • Blend until smooth and thick
  • Taste and adjust sweetness
  • Serve immediately

MayaNut™ Roasted Latte
Beverage Plant-Based 8 min
A warm, roasted drink idea using MayaNut™ flour (MayaNut powder) as a cocoa-like base.
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1–1¼ cups milk or plant milk
  • ½ tsp cinnamon (optional)
  • 1 tsp vanilla (optional)

Method
  • Whisk flour with a splash of milk

  • Serve warm

MayaNut™ Flatbreads / Corn-Blend Tortillas
Cooking Plant-Based 15 min
A simple everyday bread idea: blend MayaNut™ flour with corn flour for quick tortillas or pan-flatbreads.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup corn flour (masa harina)
  • 2–4 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • ¾ cup warm water (add slowly)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp oil (optional)
Method
  • Mix dry ingredients
  • Add water and knead to a soft dough
  • Press or roll into rounds
  • Cook on a hot pan until set

MayaNut™ Breakfast Muffins
Baking Classic 25 min
A straightforward muffin base where MayaNut™ flour adds roasted flavor. Use eggs or a simple egg substitute.
Ingredients
  • 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup MayaNut™ flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 2 eggs (or flax eggs)
  • ⅓ cup oil or melted butter
  • ½–¾ cup milk
Method
  • Mix dry ingredients
  • Whisk wet ingredients
  • Combine gently (don’t overmix)
  • Bake until set

MayaNut™ Roasted Cookies
Baking Dessert 35 min
Classic cookies where MayaNut™ flour adds warm roasted depth. Keep it simple: butter, sugar, egg, flour, and chips.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup MayaNut™ flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup butter (or plant butter)
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg (or egg substitute)
  • ½–1 cup chocolate chips
Method
  • Cream butter + sugar
  • Mix in egg and vanilla (optional)
  • Fold in dry ingredients
  • Scoop and bake until edges set
