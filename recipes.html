MayaNut™ Flour & Powder Recipes

MayaNut™ Light Flour is the default and is best for pancakes, breads, muffins, cookies, brownies, flatbreads, and other baking recipes where a lighter, more flexible flour-style use makes the most sense.

MayaNut™ Dark Roast is best for drinks, lattes, smoothies, bowls, oats, and topping-style uses where a deeper roasted flavor is the point, including sprinkling over yogurt, smoothie bowls, brownies, and similar foods.

MayaNut™ Latte

8 Min Easy Drinks

MayaNut™ Dark Roast is the right fit here because the goal is a deeper roasted, coffee-style flavor that comes through clearly in a hot drink rather than a lighter baking-style flour effect.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ Dark Roast
  • 8 oz hot milk or oat milk
  • 1 to 2 tsp brown sugar
Instructions
  • Warm the milk until steaming.
  • Whisk in MayaNut™ and brown sugar.
  • Add cinnamon if desired and serve.

MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie

5 Min Easy Smoothies

MayaNut™ Dark Roast works best in this smoothie because the deeper roasted flavor gives the blend more body and a stronger cocoa-like direction than Light Flour would.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ Dark Roast
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 3/4 cup milk, ice, 1 date
Instructions
  • Add everything to a blender.
  • Blend until smooth and creamy.
  • Adjust thickness and serve cold.

Which MayaNut™ Flour & Powder Option Should You Use?

MayaNut™ Light Flour is the default and is best for pancakes, breads, muffins, cookies, brownies, flatbreads, and baking recipes where a lighter and more flexible flour-style use makes the most sense.

MayaNut™ Dark Roast is best for drinks, smoothies, bowls, oats, and topping-style applications where the deeper roasted flavor is meant to stand out, including sprinkling over yogurt, smoothie bowls, brownies, and similar foods.

Think of MayaNut™ Light Flour as the baking-first option, and MayaNut™ Dark Roast as the roast-forward option for drinks, bowls, oats, and finishing uses.
