What is MayaNut™ flour?
MayaNut™ flour is roasted seed flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum).
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Add it to smoothies, bowls, baked goods, or everyday cooking for warm roasted depth and a more satisfying texture.
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free. Always confirm handling practices if you have dietary sensitivities.
How much MayaNut™ flour per smoothie?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons per smoothie and adjust to taste.
