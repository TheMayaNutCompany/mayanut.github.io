MayaNut™ Flour Recipes

Explore current recipe ideas built around MayaNut™ Flour, a roasted, finely milled flour made from the seeds of the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). These recipes show how MayaNut™ Flour can be mixed into smoothies, stirred into açaí bowls, baked into breakfast and dessert recipes, blended into warm drinks, or sprinkled as a topping to flavor foods and beverages.

MayaNut™ Flour Latte

8 Min Easy Drink

A warm drink recipe that shows MayaNut™ Flour as a beverage flavoring, creating a smooth, coffee-style cup with roasted depth and bold cocoa-like character.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ Flour
  • 8 oz hot milk or oat milk
  • 1 to 2 tsp brown sugar
Instructions
  • Warm the milk until steaming.
  • Whisk in MayaNut™ Flour and sugar.
  • Add cinnamon if desired and serve.

MayaNut™ Flour Cocoa-Style Smoothie

5 Min Easy Drink

A creamy smoothie that uses MayaNut™ Flour as both an ingredient and a drink flavoring, pairing especially well with frozen banana, dates, and cinnamon.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ Flour
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 3/4 cup milk, ice, 1 date
Instructions
  • Add everything to a blender.
  • Blend until smooth and creamy.
  • Adjust thickness and serve cold.

10 Min Easy Bowl

MayaNut™ Flour Overnight Oats

5 Min Easy Breakfast

A make-ahead breakfast that uses MayaNut™ Flour as a warm, roasted flavor addition in oats, especially alongside maple, chia, berries, and banana.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ Flour
  • 2/3 cup milk and 1 tsp chia
Instructions
  • Stir everything in a jar.
  • Chill overnight.
  • Top with fruit and enjoy.

Use MayaNut™ Flour across foods and beverages
