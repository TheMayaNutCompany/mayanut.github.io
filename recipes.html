MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie
Vegan Gluten-Free 5 min
A creamy, roasted cocoa-like smoothie using MayaNut™ flour for a richer, more satisfying flavor.
Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup milk of choice (or water)
  • 1–2 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa (optional)
  • 1 tbsp honey/maple (optional)
  • Pinch of salt + ice as needed
Preparation
  • Blend everything until smooth.
  • Add more liquid to thin, ice to thicken.
  • Taste, then adjust sweetness/salt.

MayaNut™ Açaí Bowl Boost
Vegan Gluten-Free 10 min
Stir MayaNut™ flour into the bowl base for a richer, more satisfying flavor that pairs with fruit. This card is formatted exactly like a product-style block.
Ingredients
  • 1 açaí packet (unsweetened)
  • ½ cup frozen berries
  • ¼–½ cup liquid (as needed)
  • 1–2 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • Toppings: banana, granola, honey (optional)
Preparation
  • Blend base to thick, spoonable texture.
  • Stir in MayaNut™ flour (or blend it in).
  • Top and serve immediately.

MayaNut™ Roasted Latte
Drink 12 min
A warm, roasted, cocoa-like drink that feels like a cozy latte—no complicated equipment required.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup milk of choice
  • 1–2 tsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1 tsp cocoa (optional)
  • Honey/maple to taste
  • Cinnamon (optional)
Preparation
  • Warm milk on low heat (do not boil).
  • Whisk in MayaNut™ flour + cocoa.
  • Simmer 2–3 min, whisking; sweeten.

MayaNut™ Chocolate Chip Cookies
Baking 22 min
Simple, classic cookies with a roasted depth from MayaNut™ flour. Keep it easy—one bowl and straightforward baking.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup MayaNut™ flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup butter (softened)
  • ½ cup brown sugar + ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 egg + 1 tsp vanilla
  • ¾ cup chocolate chips
Preparation
  • Oven: 350°F • Bake: 10–12 min.
  • Cream butter+sugars; mix in egg+vanilla.
  • Stir in dry, then chips; scoop and bake.

MayaNut™ Breakfast Muffins
Baking 32 min
Fluffy, lightly sweet muffins that highlight MayaNut™ flour’s roasted flavor—great for breakfast or snacks.
Ingredients
  • 1½ cups flour (AP or GF blend)
  • ⅓ cup MayaNut™ flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder + ½ tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ¾ cup milk (or alt milk)
  • ⅓ cup sugar or honey
  • ¼ cup oil or melted butter
Preparation
  • Oven: 375°F • Bake: 16–18 min.
  • Whisk dry; whisk wet; combine gently.
  • Fill cups ~¾ full; bake until set.

MayaNut™ Corn-Style Flatbreads
Savory 40 min
A simple savory base (tortilla-style or small flatbreads) using a MayaNut™ flour + corn flour blend.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup masa harina (or fine corn flour)
  • ¼ cup MayaNut™ flour
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¾–1 cup warm water (as needed)
  • Optional: 1 tbsp oil for softer breads
Preparation
  • Rest dough 10 min, then form small rounds.
  • Skillet: medium-high • 1–2 min per side.
  • Cover to keep soft; serve warm.
