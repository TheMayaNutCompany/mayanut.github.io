MayaNut™ Roasted Latte
Beverage Caffeine-Free 8 min
A warm, roasted drink idea using MayaNut™ flour as a cocoa-like base. Add sweetener and spices to taste.
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • Hot milk or plant milk
  • Optional: cinnamon, vanilla
  • Sweetener to taste
Method
  • Whisk flour with a splash of liquid
  • Add remaining hot milk
  • Whisk until smooth
  • Serve warm
