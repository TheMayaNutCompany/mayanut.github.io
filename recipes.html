MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie
Vegan Gluten-Free 5 min
A creamy, roasted cocoa-like smoothie using MayaNut™ flour for a richer, more satisfying flavor.
Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup milk of choice (or water)
  • 1–2 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1 tbsp cocoa (optional)
  • 1 tbsp honey/maple (optional)
  • Pinch of salt + ice as needed
Preparation
  • Blend everything until smooth.
  • Add more liquid to thin, ice to thicken.
  • Taste, then adjust sweetness/salt.

MayaNut™ Açaí Bowl Boost
Vegan Gluten-Free 10 min
Stir MayaNut™ flour into the bowl base for a richer, more satisfying flavor that pairs with fruit. This card is formatted exactly like a product-style block.

MayaNut™ Roasted Latte
Drink 12 min
A warm, roasted cocoa-like drink—simple, cozy, and surprisingly satisfying.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup milk of choice
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1–2 tsp cocoa (optional)
  • Honey/maple to taste
  • Pinch of salt + cinnamon (optional)
Preparation
  • Heat milk until steaming (do not boil).
  • Whisk in MayaNut™ flour until smooth.
  • Sweeten, season, and serve warm.

MayaNut™ + Corn Tortillas / Flatbreads
Vegan Gluten-Free 20 min
A simple savory base—MayaNut™ adds roasted depth without overpowering your toppings or fillings.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup masa harina (corn flour)
  • 2–3 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 3/4 cup warm water (as needed)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
Preparation
  • Mix dry ingredients, add water to form dough.
  • Press/roll into thin rounds.
  • Cook on hot skillet 60–90 sec/side.

MayaNut™ Breakfast Muffins
Baking 25 min
Soft, satisfying muffins where MayaNut™ flour adds a roasted depth that pairs well with cinnamon, vanilla, or chocolate chips.
Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup MayaNut™ flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg, 1 cup milk, 3 tbsp oil
  • Vanilla + pinch of salt
Preparation
  • Oven: 375°F. Bake 16–18 min.
  • Mix dry + wet separately, then combine.
  • Fill cups, bake, cool 5 min.

MayaNut™ Roasted Cookies
Baking 30 min
Classic cookies with an extra warm, roasted depth—MayaNut™ flour brings a subtle cocoa-like note.
Ingredients
  • 1 cup flour + 1/3 cup MayaNut™ flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda + pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup butter (softened)
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar + 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg + 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional)
Preparation
  • Oven: 350°F. Bake 10–12 min.
  • Cream butter+sugar, add egg+vanilla.
  • Stir in dry, scoop, bake, cool.
