MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie
Vegan Gluten-Free 5 min
Creamy, roasted, cocoa-like depth—MayaNut™ flour makes this smoothie taste richer and more satisfying without overpowering fruit. It’s an easy, gluten-free, plant-based option for busy mornings.
Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup milk (or oat/almond milk)
  • 1–2 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • Ice, optional cocoa, optional sweetener
Instructions
  • Add everything to a blender
  • Blend until creamy and smooth
  • Taste, then adjust sweetness
  • Serve cold, enjoy immediately

MayaNut™ Roasted Latte
12 min Drinks
Cozy and roasted with a gentle cocoa-like note—MayaNut™ flour adds body and depth to coffee or a caffeine-free warm drink. Great for everyday beverages on cooler nights.

MayaNut™ + Corn Tortillas (or Flatbread)
Savory 20 min Medium
A simple savory base: blend MayaNut™ flour with corn flour for warm, roasted depth that pairs with tacos, eggs, soups, and everyday cooking. The cocoa-like aroma stays subtle and smooth.
Ingredients
  • ¾ cup corn flour
  • ¼ cup MayaNut™ flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Warm water, as needed
Instructions
  • Mix dry ingredients
  • Add water to form dough
  • Press or roll thin
