FAQ
What is MayaNut™ flour?
MayaNut™ flour is roasted seed flour made from the Alicastrum tree (Brosimum alicastrum). It adds a warm, roasted depth to smoothies, bowls, baked goods, and everyday cooking.
How do you use MayaNut™ flour?
Start with 1–2 tablespoons in smoothies or bowls, or blend into baking mixes for a richer, more satisfying flavor. Adjust to taste based on your recipe.
Is MayaNut™ flour gluten-free?
MayaNut™ flour is naturally gluten-free. Always verify any added ingredients in your recipe to keep the final dish gluten-free.
What does MayaNut™ flour taste like?
It has a warm, roasted, cocoa-like flavor that adds depth and richness without being overly sweet, making recipes taste more satisfying.
Can you bake with MayaNut™ flour?
Yes. It works well in muffins, cookies, pancakes, and more—often blended with other flours depending on texture and structure.
Does MayaNut™ flour taste like cocoa?
Many people describe it as cocoa-like because of its roasted depth, but it is its own unique flavor. It pairs well with chocolate, vanilla, cinnamon, and fruit.
Can MayaNut™ flour replace cocoa powder?
It can add a cocoa-like roasted note, but it is not identical to cocoa powder. It works best as a flavor-and-body booster rather than a 1:1 cocoa replacement.
How should I store MayaNut™ flour?
Store in a cool, dry place in an airtight container. For longer storage, keep it sealed and away from heat and humidity.
Where can I buy MayaNut™ flour?
Visit the product page to see availability and updates.
What is the Alicastrum tree?
Alicastrum (Brosimum alicastrum) is the tree that produces the seeds used to make MayaNut™ flour. Learn more on the About page.
