MayaNut™ Latte

8 Min Easy Drink

The most natural first recipe for MayaNut™: a cozy coffee-style hot drink with warm roasted depth and bold cocoa-like flavor. Brown sugar works especially well here.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ powder
  • 8 oz hot milk or oat milk
  • 1 to 2 tsp brown sugar
Instructions
  • Warm the milk until steaming.
  • Whisk in MayaNut™ and brown sugar.
  • Add cinnamon if desired and serve.

MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie

5 Min Easy Drink

Creamy and rich without relying on cocoa, this smoothie uses MayaNut™ for warm roasted depth that pairs especially well with frozen banana, dates, and cinnamon.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ powder
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 3/4 cup milk, ice, 1 date
Instructions
  • Add everything to a blender.
  • Blend until smooth and creamy.
  • Adjust thickness and serve cold.

Blend MayaNut™ into the base for a richer bowl with more depth. It works especially well with banana, berries, and crunchy granola.

MayaNut™ Overnight Oats

5 Min Easy Breakfast

A fast make-ahead breakfast where MayaNut™ adds roasted depth and works especially well with maple, chia, banana, and berries.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ powder
  • 2/3 cup milk and 1 tsp chia
Instructions
  • Stir everything in a jar.
  • Chill overnight.
  • Top with fruit and eat.
