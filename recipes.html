MayaNut™ Latte

8 MIN GLUTEN-FREE
Download this recipe
The most common way to use MayaNut™: a cozy, coffee-style hot drink with warm roasted depth and gentle cocoa-like notes—caffeine-free by default.
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ powder
  • 1 cup hot milk (or milk alternative)
  • 1 tsp honey/maple (optional)
  • Cinnamon or vanilla (optional)
Instructions
  • Warm milk until steaming.
  • Whisk in MayaNut™ powder.
  • Sweeten/spice, serve.
