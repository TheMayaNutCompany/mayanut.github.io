MayaNut™ Cocoa-Style Smoothie

5 MIN VEGAN GLUTEN-FREE
Download this recipe
A creamy, cocoa-like smoothie made with MayaNut™ flour for warm roasted depth, richer flavor, and a naturally more satisfying texture.
Ingredients
  • 1–2 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • 1 banana (or frozen fruit)
  • 1 cup milk of choice
  • Ice + cinnamon (optional)
Instructions
  • Add everything to a blender.
  • Blend until smooth.

Stir MayaNut™ flour into the bowl base for a richer, more satisfying flavor that pairs beautifully with fruit, granola, and toppings.

  • 1 tbsp MayaNut™ flour
  • Frozen banana + berries

Instructions
